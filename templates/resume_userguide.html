Application User Guide

Master our AI-powered features, navigate the Glassmorphism design, and optimize your resume for high ATS scores.

Design Overview

Our application utilizes a modern Glassmorphism aesthetic combined with vibrant gradient accents. This design philosophy emphasizes frosted glass textures, ambient background blobs, and lightweight translucent panels that feel responsive and alive.

  • Glass Panels: Most structural elements and cards use a translucent background with background-blur, providing a feeling of depth and hierarchy.
  • Micro-animations: Subtle hover effects and bouncy animations encourage interaction and make the interface feel premium and engaging.
  • Typography: We use Outfit for headings to give a sleek appearance and Inter for easily legible body text.

Core Features

Job Match

Upload your resume (PDF/DOCX) and a target Job Description. Our Sarvam AI integration will analyze the documents and output a structured match score with precise feedback on gaps.

Interview

After matching, dynamically generate an assessment. The AI asks contextual questions based on your resume and evaluates your text answers in real-time.

Analytics

Review your past interview performance. Break down answer strengths, view your total score, and identify areas of improvement through detailed feedback.

Chatbot Assistant

Aria, your virtual assistant, is available securely on every page via the floating widget at the bottom right. Ask her for help or quick navigation.

How to Use the App

1

Start a Job Match

Navigate to Job Match. Upload your latest resume and paste the job description of a role you are targeting. Click "Analyze" to see your alignment score and missing skills.

2

Proceed to Interview

From the Match Result page, click "Start Interview" to enter the simulation phase. The system generates specialized questions based on what was missing from your resume context.

3

Answer the AI Questions

Converse directly with the AI in the Interview dashboard. Provide detailed, STAR-method answers. Submit your answer to proceed to the next question.

4

Review Analytics

Head over to the Analytics page once the interview concludes. See aggregate scores and precise, constructive feedback for each answer given.

Tips for High ATS Scores

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan resumes for relevance before a human ever sees them. To improve your match rates significantly within our system (and real-world software):

Key Optimization Strategies

  • Exact Keyword Matching: Use the exact terms found in the Job Description. If they ask for "Customer Relationship Management", do not just write "CRM". Use both: "Customer Relationship Management (CRM)".

  • Action Verbs: Start bullet points with strong action verbs: Orchestrated, Developed, Implemented, Spearheaded, Optimized.

  • Quantifiable Metrics: "Increased sales by 25% over 6 months" ranks drastically higher than "Helped increase sales". Wrap your achievements in numbers, dollars, or percentages.

  • Standard Headings: Use traditional formatting like "Work Experience", "Education", and "Skills". Creative titles like "My Journey" will break the parser.

  • Avoid Complex Layouts: Dual-column resumes, heavy graphics/tables, and non-standard fonts fail in ATS parsers. Stick to a clean, single-column chronological structure.

The STAR Method

When formatting your bullet points or answering the AI interview questions, always use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Conclude your points with the impact you made.
