FAQ

Quick answers.

Still curious? Our team replies within a day.

Is the free tier truly free? expand_more

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever — including 3 monthly mock interviews, communication lab access, and weekly Readiness Index updates.

How does the AI keep my data safe? expand_more

All recordings, transcripts, and resumes are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We are GDPR-compliant and you can delete every artifact with one click.

Can colleges license Career Buddy campus-wide? expand_more

Absolutely. Institutional licensing includes SSO, white-label branding, a placement-cell dashboard, and cohort-level analytics. Pricing scales with enrollment.

How are candidates "verified" for Employer Connect? expand_more

Verification = a Readiness Index above 75, plus a completed mock-interview library (3+ sessions) and an ATS-validated resume. Recruiters see the full evidence trail.
