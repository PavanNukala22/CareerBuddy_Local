    Module 01 · Foundations

    What is Journey and Ecosystem Mapping?

    Start from zero: journeys, touchpoints, channels and the difference between maps, blueprints and ecosystems.

    🗺️ What is a Customer Journey?

    A customer journey is the full sequence of steps, thoughts and feelings a person goes through to reach a goal with an organisation — from first awareness to long-term loyalty. A journey map visualises it.

    🔴 Screen-only view

    We optimise the app's sign-up flow and ignore the call to the helpdesk, the WhatsApp reminder and the printed certificate.

    🟢 End-to-end view

    We see every touchpoint, online and offline, and fix the moments that really hurt.

    🧩 Key Terms

    TouchpointAny point of contact: ad, website, app, call, email, in-person.
    ChannelThe medium for a touchpoint (web, app, phone, store, social).
    OmnichannelChannels connected so the experience is seamless.
    MultichannelMany channels but not integrated.
    Persona / actorThe person whose journey is mapped.
    Moment of truthA decisive moment that shapes opinion.
    Pain pointFrustration or obstacle.
    OpportunityIdea to improve a moment.
    Frontstage / backstageWhat the customer sees vs the internal work behind it.

    🗂️ Types of Maps

    MapWhat it showsBest for
    Current-state journey mapHow things are todayFinding pain points
    Future-state mapIdeal experienceVision and roadmap
    Day-in-the-lifeA person's whole day, not just your productDiscovering context
    Experience mapGeneric human journey independent of brandNew opportunity areas
    Service blueprintJourney + backstage processes, systems, peopleOperations and delivery
    Ecosystem mapAll actors, services and relationshipsStrategy, partnerships

    Module 03 · Omnichannel Touchpoints

    Omnichannel Touchpoints Beyond Screens

    Research physical, human and offline moments alongside digital ones.

    📡 Touchpoint Inventory

    GroupExamples
    Digital ownedWebsite, app, email, push, in-app chat
    Digital earned / socialReviews, YouTube, WhatsApp communities
    HumanCall centre, counsellor, trainer, field sales
    PhysicalCertificate print, study kit, centre visit, events
    Third-partyPayment gateway, bank, courier, employer HR
    Documents and policyInvoices, T&Cs, refund rules
    EXAMPLE · Beyond screens

    A learner pays by UPI. The bank holds the payment 2 hours; the platform shows "failed". She calls support, which cannot see the bank status. The worst moment occurs outside the product.

    🔗 Channel Handoffs

    • Map transitions: app → call, web → store, email → WhatsApp.
    • Check continuity: does the next channel know the context? (No repeating the story.)
    • Check consistency: same price, tone, and policy in every channel.
    • Measure channel switching as a signal of friction.

    🔴 Multichannel

    App, call centre and email each have their own data. Customer repeats the issue 3 times.

    🟢 Omnichannel

    Shared case history and identity. Agent sees what happened in the app and picks up.

    🔬 Research Methods for Touchpoints

    MethodUse
    Contextual inquiry / shadowingSee offline moments
    Diary studiesCapture moments across channels over time
    Mystery shopping / self-serviceExperience the journey as a customer
    Support ticket and call analysisFind frequent failure points
    Analytics and CRM funnelsQuantify drop-offs and channel switches
    Frontline staff interviewsHear what customers tell staff

    💡

    🌐 Ecosystem Mapping

    An ecosystem map shows all the actors (customers, staff, partners, regulators, competitors) and the flows between them: money, information, services, trust.

    Module 05 · Expert Practice

    Expert Journey and Ecosystem Strategy

    Run mapping at scale: workshops, governance, quantification and action.

    👥 Facilitating Mapping Workshops

    • Invite cross-functional owners (product, support, marketing, ops, sales).
    • Start with research evidence, then map with sticky notes in silence before discussion.
    • Time-box stages; capture assumptions on a separate colour.
    • Finish with dot-voting on opportunities and assign owners.

    📊 Quantifying Journeys

    TechniqueHow
    Overlay analyticsAdd drop-off %, time, contact rate per stage
    Emotion curve from surveyAsk 1–5 mood per stage
    Cost of frictionSupport cost + lost revenue per pain point
    Opportunity scoringImpact × frequency × feasibility
    EXAMPLE · Cost of one pain point

    2,000 learners per month hit "OTP delay"; 18% abandon; each is worth ₹6,000. Monthly loss ≈ 2,000 × 18% × ₹6,000 = ₹21.6 lakh. The fix now has a business case.

    🏛️ Governance and Living Maps

    • Assign a journey owner per journey, not per channel.
    • Review maps quarterly; update after launches or incidents.
    • Link each pain point to a ticket and a metric.
    • Store maps in a shared, searchable repository.

    🧠 Common Pitfalls

    🔴 Poster maps

    Beautiful, printed, pinned to a wall, never used.

    🟢 Working maps

    Evidence-based, quantified, tied to owners and roadmap items, updated regularly.

    🎓 Scenario

    Learners complain about enrolment. Sales agents, a payment gateway, WhatsApp reminders and a call centre all play a part. You must map the end-to-end journey and recommend fixes.

    1️⃣ Task 1 · Scope

    ✍️ Define the persona, scenario and stages for the map.
    Model answer: Persona: Ravi, 29, working professional. Scenario: enrol in a data analytics course using his phone. Stages: Aware → Compare → Decide → Pay → Onboard → Get help.

    2️⃣ Task 2 · Touchpoints

    ✍️ List 6 touchpoints, including at least 3 outside the app.
    Model answer: Instagram ad, WhatsApp sales agent, website pricing page, payment gateway/UPI app, bank SMS, call-centre agent, welcome email.

    3️⃣ Task 3 · Blueprint

    Q. Learner sees "payment failed" but money was debited. Which layer is the root cause?
    • A Backstage/support process: gateway status not synced with LMS
    • B Customer action only
    • C Colour
    • D Logo

    4️⃣ Task 4 · Opportunity

    ✍️ Estimate the cost of the pain point: 1,500 payers/month, 12% fail, ₹5,000 value. Then propose a fix.
    Model answer: Cost: 1,500 × 12% × ₹5,000 = ₹9 lakh/month. Fix: real-time gateway status check in app, automatic retry, WhatsApp confirmation, and a support dashboard showing payment state.

    Module 07 · Interview Preparation

    Interview Preparation

    Click a question to reveal a model answer.

    🗺️ Concepts

    Journey map vs service blueprint vs ecosystem map?
    Journey map: the customer's experience over time. Blueprint: adds frontstage/backstage processes and systems. Ecosystem map: all actors and flows around a service.
    Multichannel vs omnichannel?
    Multichannel offers many channels separately; omnichannel connects them so identity and context persist.
    What are the layers of a journey map?
    Actor and scenario, stages, actions, touchpoints, thoughts, emotions, pain points and opportunities.

    🔬 Method

    How do you build a map from evidence?
    Scope the persona and goal, gather interviews, diaries, analytics and support data, draft, validate with users and teams, then prioritise and assign owners.
    How do you research offline touchpoints?
    Shadowing, contextual inquiry, diaries, mystery shopping, call analysis and frontline staff interviews.
    How do you quantify a journey?
    Overlay conversion, time, contact rate and emotion scores, then translate pain points into cost.

    🏛️ Impact

    Why do many journey maps fail?
    They are opinion-based, not owned, not linked to metrics, and never updated.
    How do you facilitate a mapping workshop?
    Bring evidence, silent mapping first, time-boxed stages, mark assumptions, and finish with voting and owners.
