    Module 01 · Foundations

    What is Art Direction?

    Start from zero: what an art director does, how it differs from design, and the main kinds of creative direction.

    🎬 What is Design Art Direction?

    Art direction is deciding how something should look and feel and guiding a team to deliver it. An art director sets the visual concept, style, imagery and mood so campaigns, hero sections and marketing assets tell one clear story.

    🔴 No direction

    Each designer, photographer and illustrator makes their own choices. Outputs look unrelated.

    🟢 With direction

    A shared concept, mood board and rules produce a cohesive set of assets people recognise.

    🧭 Art Direction vs Related Roles

    RoleFocusOutput
    Visual designerCrafts screens and assetsUI, graphics
    Art directorVisual concept and style for a projectMood boards, style frames, briefs
    Creative directorOverall creative vision across copy and visualsCampaign strategy, approvals
    Brand designerIdentity systemLogo, guidelines

    📖 Key Terms and Types

    Creative briefDocument stating goal, audience, message, deliverables, constraints.
    ConceptThe central idea that drives visuals.
    Mood boardCollage of references to define mood, colour and style.
    Style frameA fully designed key frame showing final look.
    Hero elementThe main visual at the top of a page or campaign.
    Shot listList of photos/shots to capture with framing and notes.
    MoodtoneEmotional atmosphere: calm, energetic, premium.

    Module 02 · Mood Boards & Research

    Mood Boards and Visual Research

    Turn a brief into a visual direction through research, references and style frames.

    📝 Reading a Creative Brief

    • Goal: what must change (sign-ups, awareness)?
    • Audience: who, what they like, where they see it.
    • Message: one main idea.
    • Tone: 3 adjectives (e.g. warm, ambitious, clear).
    • Deliverables and limits: sizes, formats, budget, deadline.

    🖼️ Building a Mood Board

    Figure 2.1 · Mood board process
    IncludeWhy
    Photography and illustrationSubject, crop, lighting
    Colour swatchesPalette direction
    Type samplesPersonality
    Texture and materialsTactile feeling
    Anti-references ("not this")Clarifies boundaries
    💡
    Tools: Pinterest, Milanote, Figma/FigJam, Behance, Savee, Are.na. Credit and check licences before using references in final work.

    🎨 From Mood Board to Style Frame

    A style frame applies the direction to one real layout (e.g. the landing page hero) so stakeholders judge the actual look, not abstract ideas.

    EXAMPLE · Direction statement

    "Warm and ambitious. Natural window light, real learners, navy and amber palette, rounded shapes, generous space. Avoid stock-style handshakes and cold blue gradients."

    Q. What does an anti-reference show?

    • B The budget
    • C The deadline
    • D What to avoid

    Photography and Imagery Direction

    Direct photoshoots, select imagery and keep a consistent visual language.

    📷 Types of Imagery

    TypeFeelUse
    Documentary / candidAuthentic, realTestimonials, culture
    PortraitPersonal, humanInstructors, team
    Product / lifestyleAspirationalApp in use
    Abstract / textureAtmosphericBackgrounds
    IllustrationFriendly, flexibleConcepts, onboarding
    3D renderModern, polishedHero, features

    🎥 Directing a Shoot

    • Write a shot list with subject, framing (wide, medium, close-up), angle and purpose.
    • Define lighting: natural soft, high-key bright, low-key dramatic.
    • Plan copy space for headlines and cropping for each format (16:9, 1:1, 9:16).
    • Cast for inclusion and authenticity; get model releases.
    • Provide a call sheet: time, location, props, wardrobe colours.
    Depth of fieldShallow = blurred background; deep = all in focus.
    Rule of thirdsPlace subjects on grid intersections.
    Colour gradeConsistent colour treatment across images.
    RetouchingCleaning and enhancing without losing realism.

    ⚖️ Selecting and Licensing

    SourceNote
    CommissionedOwn the rights; custom
    Stock (royalty-free)Check licence, avoid clichés
    AI-generatedCheck policy, bias, rights and disclosure
    User-generatedNeeds permission
    ⚠️
    Always record licence, usage limits, model releases and expiry dates.

    🔎 Visual Rules for Marketing

    • One focal point, one message and one call-to-action per asset.
    • Safe areas: keep text away from edges and platform UI overlays.
    • Consistent colour grade, type and layout motif across set.
    • Test variations (A/B) of image and headline.

    Module 05 · Expert Creative Direction

    Expert-Level Creative Direction

    Lead teams, present concepts, give feedback and measure creative impact.

    🧠 Concept Development

    • Start with an insight about the audience, then a big idea, then execution.
    • Explore at least 3 distinct directions; vary risk (safe, bold, unexpected).
    • Test concepts with the brief: does it fit goal, audience and brand?
    • Name each direction and sell it with a one-line rationale.

    🗣️ Presenting and Feedback

    PracticeHow
    Present the problem firstBrief → insight → concept → proof
    Show in contextMockups on real screens/sites
    Give direction, not fixes"More energy" with references rather than "make it red"
    Align stakeholders earlySign-off gates: concept, style frame, final

    👥 Leading a Creative Team

    Creative brief for othersShort, visual and clear with examples.
    Review rhythmWeekly crits; early work-in-progress checks.
    Quality barDefine what "done" looks like with samples.
    Credit and growthGive ownership and learning opportunities.

    📊 Measuring Creative Impact

    MetricTells you
    CTR, conversionEffectiveness of visual and CTA
    Time on page / scroll depthEngagement with hero and story
    Brand recall testsMemorability
    A/B testsWhich direction performs

    Practice: Direct an LMS Launch Campaign

    Plan creative direction for a real campaign.

    0 / 0 correct

    🚀 Scenario

    Your LMS is launching "Skills 2026", targeting working professionals aged 25–40 who want to upskill. Tone: motivating, human, modern. Assets: landing hero, 6 social posts, an email header and a 15-second video.

    1️⃣ Task 1 · Brief

    ✍️ Write a 4-line creative brief (goal, audience, message, tone).
    Model answer: Goal: 5,000 sign-ups in 30 days. Audience: professionals 25–40 upskilling after work. Message: "Learn something that moves your career." Tone: motivating, human, clear.

    2️⃣ Task 2 · Mood board

    ✍️ List 6 elements for the mood board and 2 anti-references.
    Model answer: Natural evening light photos, real learners at laptops, navy/amber palette, rounded shapes, bold sans type, subtle paper texture. Avoid: stock handshakes, cold blue tech gradients.

    3️⃣ Task 3 · Hero

    Q. Best hero pattern for a clear sign-up message?
    • A Blank page
    • B Text-led or image-led hero with headline and one CTA
    • C Five competing CTAs
    • D Only a full-screen video with no text

    4️⃣ Task 4 · Shot list

    ✍️ Write 3 shots for the photoshoot.
    Model answer: 1) Medium shot: learner at home desk, window light, copy space right. 2) Close-up: hands on keyboard with course UI. 3) Wide: study group smiling in a bright cafe; crops 16:9 and 1:1.

    Module 07 · Interview Preparation

    Interview Preparation

    Click a question to reveal a model answer.

    🎬 Fundamentals

    What does an art director do?
    Defines the visual concept and style of a project and guides designers, photographers and illustrators to deliver a cohesive result.
    How do you start a project?
    Read the brief, research audience and competitors, build a mood board, explore directions and align with stakeholders.
    What is a mood board for?
    To communicate mood, colour, style and imagery before production, so everyone shares the same direction.

    📷 Craft

    How do you direct a photoshoot?
    Shot list, lighting and styling plan, copy space, casting, call sheet and review of selects against the brief.
    How do you make a strong hero?
    One message, one focal point, clear CTA, strong image or visual metaphor and a tested layout for all screen sizes.
    How do you handle imagery licences?
    Record source, licence, usage rights, model releases and expiry; prefer commissioned or properly licensed assets.

    🤝 Leadership

    How do you give feedback to designers?
    Tie it to the brief, describe what you see, give direction with references and let them solve the how.
    Stakeholder dislikes the direction. What now?
    Return to the goal and audience insight, show evidence or tests, offer options and agree on a decision criterion.
    How do you measure creative success?
    Compare to goal metrics: CTR, conversion, engagement, recall and A/B results.
