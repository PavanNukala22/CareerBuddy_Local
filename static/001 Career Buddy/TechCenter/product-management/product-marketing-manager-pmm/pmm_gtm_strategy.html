    Module 01

    Go-To-Market Strategy for Product Marketing Managers

    Tiered launch orchestration, sales collateral development, and pricing and packaging recommendations that take a product from ready to adopted and paid for.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    GTM strategy is the PMM's commercial craft: pick the right motion, package and price the offer, orchestrate the launch and prove it worked with pipeline and revenue.

    🚀
    Launch tiers
    Right-sized orchestration.
    📄
    Collateral
    Assets sales actually use.
    💲
    Pricing
    Packaging and value.
    📈
    GTM motion
    Channels, funnel, results.
    💡
    GTM strategy answers three questions: who we sell to, how we reach them, and what we charge. Launch is the first test of the answer.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Choose a GTM motion and plan segments, channels and goals.
    2. Orchestrate tiered launches across product, sales, CS and marketing.
    3. Develop sales collateral for each buying stage.
    4. Recommend pricing and packaging using value and market evidence.
    5. Run launch campaigns and funnels with measurable targets.
    6. Coordinate cross-functional timelines and risks.
    7. Measure launch outcomes and iterate the GTM.

    textROI CALCULATOR EXAMPLE
Inputs: 4 hours/week saved per planner at $50/hour
Only planners are affected, so use 15 planners x 4 hours x 52 weeks x $50 = $156,000/year
Add avoided overtime $24,000 -> benefits $180,000. Cost $36,000/year -> net $144,000; ROI = 144/36 = 400%; payback = 36,000/180,000 x 12 = 2.4 months.
Always state assumptions so buyers can edit them.
    💡
    Credible ROI uses conservative, editable assumptions. Buyers discount any number they cannot inspect.

    Pricing and Packaging Recommendations

    Value-based pricing, packaging tiers, price metrics, competitive benchmarks, discounting and testing price changes.

    💲 Pricing Approaches

    ApproachHowStrengthRisk
    Cost-plusCost plus marginSimpleIgnores value
    Competitor-basedAnchor to alternativesMarket awareRace to bottom
    Value-basedShare of customer valueCaptures valueNeeds value evidence
    Usage-basedPay for consumptionAligns to valueRevenue variability
    Freemium / trialFree entryLow frictionConversion burden
    textVALUE-BASED PRICE RANGE
Customer value: $180,000 per year in benefits (from ROI model)
Common guidance: price at 10% to 25% of value -> $18,000 to $45,000 per year
Competitive alternatives: $20,000 to $35,000. Recommended list price: $30,000/year for the core plan, leaving customers 5x value.

    🧮 Intermediate: Price Change Math

    textPRICE INCREASE SCENARIO
Current: 1,000 customers x $100 = $100,000/month
Proposed: $110 (+10%); expected churn from price +4% -> 960 customers
New revenue = 960 x 110 = $105,600 (+5.6%)
Break-even churn for +10% price: customers needed = 100,000/110 = 909 -> up to 9.1% loss tolerated.

DISCOUNT MATH: a 20% discount needs 25% more volume to keep revenue the same (0.8 x 1.25 = 1.0).
    Discount rulePractice
    Approval levelsRep up to 10%, manager 20%, VP beyond
    Give-getsDiscount for multi-year or case study
    Floor priceProtect margins
    TrackDiscount rate by segment

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Product Marketing interviews about go-to-market strategy, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.How do you tier launches?
    By impact on revenue, brand and customers; larger tiers get full campaigns, smaller get release notes and docs.
    IQ2.Walk me through a tier 1 launch.
    Brief, messaging and pricing, assets, enablement, readiness review, go/no-go, launch, war room, retro.
    IQ3.How do you decide GTM motion?
    Based on ACV, buying complexity, time to value and channels: PLG, sales-led or hybrid.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.What sales collateral matters most?
    Deck, one-pager, case studies, ROI tool, demo script and objection guide mapped to buying stages.
    IQ5.How do you approach pricing?
    Value-based with competitor benchmarks and segment willingness to pay; test and model scenarios.
    IQ6.How do you design packaging?
    Good-better-best tiers fenced by value with aligned price metric and optional add-ons.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.How do you evaluate a price increase?
    Model revenue and churn scenarios, segment, grandfather, communicate value and monitor.
    IQ8.How do you measure a launch?
    Scorecard across awareness, engagement, pipeline, revenue, adoption and enablement at 30/60/90 days.
    IQ9.How do you work with sales?
    Co-create messaging, enable, gather field feedback and measure usage and win rate.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.A launch underperforms. What next?
    Diagnose the funnel, adjust channels and offers, fix handoffs and document learnings.
    IQ11.How do you manage launch risk?
    Readiness gates, staged rollouts, war room, clear owners and decision rights.
    IQ12.Tell me about a launch you led.
    Describe goals, plan, challenges, results and lessons.
