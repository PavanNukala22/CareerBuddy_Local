    Module 01

    Market Intelligence for Product Marketing Managers

    Win/loss analysis, competitor monitoring and customer reference pipeline development: turning market signals and customer proof into positioning, sales wins and product decisions.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Market intelligence is the PMM's radar and memory. It tells the company why it wins, what competitors are doing and which customers can prove it.

    🏆
    Win/loss
    Why we win and lose.
    🔭
    Competitor watch
    Signals and response.
    🤝
    References
    Proof pipeline.
    🧠
    Insights
    Decisions from intelligence.
    💡
    Market intelligence is only valuable when it changes a decision: a message, a price, a roadmap item or a sales play.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Design and run a win/loss program with interviews and CRM data.
    2. Analyze win/loss patterns and quantify reasons.
    3. Build a competitor monitoring system with signal tiers.
    4. Produce competitive briefs and alerts for sales and product.
    5. Build and manage a customer reference and advocacy pipeline.
    6. Use intelligence to improve positioning, pricing and enablement.
    7. Handle ethics, legality and bias in competitive intelligence.

    ElementStandard
    ScopeDeals above a threshold, both wins and losses, including no-decision

    TopicExample questions

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Product Marketing interviews about market intelligence, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.How do you run a win/loss program?
    Define scope, use neutral interviews and CRM data, code reasons, quantify patterns, share insights and drive actions.
    IQ2.Why not rely on rep-reported reasons?
    Reps have bias; buyers often give different reasons than the CRM records. Interviews reveal true criteria.
    IQ3.How many interviews do you need?
    About 15 to 25 per quarter, mixing wins, losses and no-decisions; report counts and note when directional.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.How do you monitor competitors?
    Tier competitors; track websites, releases, jobs, reviews, news and deal intel; triage; alert; update cards.
    IQ5.What is in a competitive alert?
    What happened, implication, deal impact, actions, owner and confidence.
    IQ6.How do you ensure ethical intelligence?
    Use public sources, be honest, document, avoid confidential material and consult legal.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.How do you build a reference program?
    Identify happy customers, qualify, ask with value, enable, match to deals, recognize and avoid fatigue.
    IQ8.How do you measure reference impact?
    Utilization, coverage, win rate with and without references, with attention to correlation.
    IQ9.How do you turn intelligence into action?
    Synthesize insights with recommendations, assign owners and measure outcomes.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.How do you handle a surprise competitor launch?
    Verify, assess exposed deals, alert in 48 hours, update cards and brief stakeholders.
    IQ11.How do you measure the intelligence function?
    Win rate trend, time to alert, usage, coverage and decisions influenced.
    IQ12.Tell me about an insight that changed a decision.
    Describe the intelligence, recommendation, decision and outcome.
