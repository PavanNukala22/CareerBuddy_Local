    Module 01

    Commercial Enablement for Product Marketing Managers

    Training sales and customer success teams on product differentiators and handling sales objections, so every customer conversation is confident, consistent and effective.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Commercial enablement converts positioning into field performance. The PMM trains sales and customer success to explain differentiators, handle objections and win and grow accounts.

    🎓
    Programs
    Design onboarding and launch training.
    ⭐
    Differentiators
    Talk tracks that stick.
    🛡️
    Objections
    Handle concerns with proof.
    📈
    Impact
    Ramp, win rate, expansion.
    💡
    Enablement is judged by behavior change in the field, not by attendance at a training session.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Design enablement programs for sales and customer success.
    2. Teach product differentiators with talk tracks and demos.
    3. Build objection-handling frameworks and practice them.
    4. Enable customer success for adoption, renewal and expansion.
    5. Use certification, role play and coaching to reinforce learning.
    6. Measure enablement impact on ramp, win rate and retention.
    7. Run enablement operations: content, cadence and feedback loops.

    Teaching Product Differentiators

    Differentiator talk tracks, discovery tie-ins, demo storytelling, proof, competitive comparisons and customer-specific tailoring.

    ⭐ Differentiator Talk Tracks

    ElementContent
    Customer problemThe pain in customer words
    DifferentiatorWhat we do that alternatives do not
    ValueOutcome for the customer
    ProofCustomer story, data, third party
    QuestionA discovery question that surfaces the need
    textTALK TRACK: Real-time capacity forecasting
Discovery question: "How do you know today whether your team can take on a new project without missing dates?"
Problem: Most teams rely on spreadsheets updated weekly, so overload shows up too late.
Differentiator: We connect pipeline and staffing in real time and forecast overload before it happens.
Value: Teams stop overcommitting and hit more deadlines.
Proof: Delta Services cut missed deadlines 31% in six months.
Bridge: "Would it help to see your own team plan in this view?"

    Module 04

    Handling Sales Objections

    Common objections, LAER and other frameworks, objection libraries, proof, role play practice and escalation.

    🛡️ Objection Frameworks

    FrameworkStepsUse
    LAERListen, Acknowledge, Explore, RespondGeneral objections
    ACEAcknowledge, Clarify, Explain with proofQuick handling
    Feel-Felt-FoundEmpathize, relate, share outcomeEmotional concerns
    Question-firstAsk what is behind the objectionVague objections
    textOBJECTION: "Your price is higher than Competitor X."
Listen: let the buyer finish; take notes.
Acknowledge: "That is a fair point; cost matters."
Explore: "Which parts of the price concern you? How are you comparing the two?"
Respond: "X is priced per seat; our plan includes forecasting and CRM sync that customers otherwise buy separately. Delta Services saved $144k net in year one. May I walk through the total cost with you?"

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Product Marketing interviews about commercial enablement, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.What makes enablement effective?
    Needs-based design, behavior objectives, practice, coaching, certification and measurement of field results.
    IQ2.How do you train sales on a launch?
    Brief, personas, differentiators, demo, pricing, objections, certification and ongoing office hours.
    IQ3.How do you teach differentiators?
    Talk tracks with problem, differentiator, value, proof and a discovery question, practiced in role play.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.How do you handle objections?
    Use LAER, an objection library from real calls, proof assets and practice scenarios.
    IQ5.How do you measure enablement?
    Kirkpatrick levels: reaction, learning, behavior, results; ramp, win rate, recall and attach rate.
    IQ6.How do you reduce ramp time?
    Structured 30/60/90 onboarding, certification, shadowing and coaching.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.How do you enable customer success?
    Playbooks for adoption, renewal, expansion and risk; QBR templates and talk tracks.
    IQ8.Reps forget training quickly. What do you do?
    Add role play, manager coaching, micro-learning and call reviews; measure usage.
    IQ9.How do you handle competitor FUD?
    Verify claims, publish response guides with proof, train reps to ask for specifics.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.How do you keep content fresh?
    Owners, review dates, field feedback and a content hub.
    IQ11.How do you prove impact?
    Cohort comparisons, baselines and multiple metrics while acknowledging other factors.
    IQ12.Tell me about an enablement program you built.
    Describe needs, design, delivery, measurement and results.
