    Module 01

    Roadmap Integration for Technical Product Managers

    How a TPM manages platform products: developer platforms, developer experience (DX), internal data pipelines and platform infrastructure, and integrates them into company roadmaps.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Roadmap integration for a TPM means making platform and infrastructure work visible, valued and sequenced alongside customer features. It requires customers, metrics and clear stories of business impact.

    🛠️
    Developer platforms
    Treat internal and external platforms as products.
    😊
    Developer experience
    Measure and improve how developers feel and perform.
    🔀
    Data pipelines
    Own data products, quality and contracts.
    🗺️
    Platform roadmaps
    Balance enablement, reliability and customer features.
    💡
    A platform is a product whose customers are other teams. Success means their speed and quality improve, not that the platform ships features.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Define platform products with customers, value propositions and adoption goals.
    2. Measure developer experience with DORA, SPACE and developer surveys.
    3. Manage internal data pipelines as products with SLAs, contracts and quality checks.
    4. Plan infrastructure roadmaps: migrations, reliability, cost and security.
    5. Prioritize platform work against customer features using value and risk.
    6. Build adoption: docs, onboarding, golden paths, support and communication.
    7. Report platform outcomes in business terms.

    Real-World Scenarios & Troubleshooting

    Case studies: a platform nobody adopts, an unfunded infrastructure upgrade, a data pipeline outage and a stalled migration.

    🏜️ Case Study: The Platform Nobody Adopts

    A new deployment platform launched six months ago has 15% adoption; teams keep their own scripts.

    Figure 8.1 · Raising platform adoption
    ✅
    Outcome: missing custom-domain support and slow onboarding were the blockers; after fixes and a migration squad, adoption reached 70% in two quarters.

    💰 Case Study: Unfunded Infrastructure Upgrade

    1. Translate the upgrade into business risk: end of support, incident probability, compliance.
    2. Quantify cost of doing nothing.
    3. Offer a phased plan and capacity share.
    4. Present to leadership with options.
    Figure 8.2 · Winning funding

    🚨 Troubleshooting: Data Pipeline Outage

    StepAction
    1Identify affected datasets and consumers (lineage)
    2Communicate status and expected recovery
    3Backfill missing data once fixed
    4Add alerts, tests and contract checks
    5Run a blameless review

    🐢 Scenario: A Stalled Migration

    40 of 60 teams migrated; the last 20 are stuck. Segment the long tail by blocker, offer hands-on migration squads, set a deprecation date with a sponsor, track weekly, and shut down old systems on schedule.

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Technical Product Manager interviews about platforms and roadmaps, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.What is different about managing a platform product?
    Customers are other teams or developers, value shows as their speed and quality, adoption is earned through self-service, docs and trust, and success is measured by outcomes like lead time and reliability.
    IQ2.How do you measure developer experience?
    Use DORA metrics, SPACE dimensions, developer surveys, friction logs, build and onboarding times, and support ticket trends.
    IQ3.How do you drive platform adoption?
    Interview teams, fix blockers, provide golden paths and docs, pilot with friendly teams, offer migration help, share results and make the platform the easy default.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.How do you prioritize platform vs feature work?
    Score on leverage, risk reduction, time saved, enablement and urgency, agree a capacity policy, and communicate trade-offs and outcomes.
    IQ5.What is a data contract?
    An agreement between producers and consumers on schema, freshness, quality and change policy, with an owner and SLA.
    IQ6.How do you manage a large migration?
    Define the why, inventory, pilot, plan waves, run both in parallel, enable teams with tooling, set a deprecation date with a sponsor and retire the old system.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.How do you build a roadmap for infrastructure?
    Organize by outcomes such as reliability, security, cost and enablement, link initiatives to metrics, sequence by dependencies and review quarterly.
    IQ8.How do you get funding for invisible work?
    Translate to business risk and value, quantify cost of delay, offer phased options and show leading metrics.
    IQ9.How do you avoid becoming a bottleneck as a platform team?
    Prioritize self-service, automation, composable capabilities, clear docs and an enabling operating model.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.What metrics show platform success?
    Adoption rate, time to first success, self-service ratio, developer satisfaction, reliability and business impact like lead time and incidents.
    IQ11.How do you handle a data pipeline outage?
    Use lineage to identify consumers, communicate, restore and backfill, add checks and alerts, and run a blameless review.
    IQ12.Tell me about a platform you grew.
    Describe the customer problem, discovery, roadmap, adoption tactics, metrics and outcomes, and lessons learned.
