    Module 01

    Technical Discovery for Technical Product Managers

    How a TPM scopes non-functional requirements: latency, throughput, availability, security and compliance, turning vague quality wishes into measurable targets.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Technical discovery turns vague quality expectations into numbers engineers can design for and testers can verify. For a TPM, scoping NFRs early prevents costly rework, outages and compliance failures.

    ⏱️
    Latency
    Define how fast is fast enough, with percentiles.
    🚀
    Throughput
    Size for requests, data and growth.
    🟢
    Availability
    Set SLOs, error budgets and recovery goals.
    🔐
    Security & compliance
    Capture controls, privacy and regulatory duties.
    💡
    Non-functional requirements are product requirements. Users feel slowness and outages as clearly as missing features.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Elicit and write measurable NFRs from business goals and user expectations.
    2. Define latency targets with percentiles and budgets across components.
    3. Estimate throughput and capacity with assumptions and headroom.
    4. Set availability targets: SLI, SLO, SLA, error budget, RTO and RPO.
    5. Scope security and compliance: threat modeling, privacy, GDPR, SOC 2, PCI, HIPAA.
    6. Trade off NFRs against cost and delivery time.
    7. Verify NFRs through load tests, chaos tests and monitoring.

    ⏱️ Basic: Why Percentiles Beat Averages

    Figure 2.1 · Latency percentiles
    text10 requests (ms): 100, 110, 120, 120, 130, 140, 150, 160, 900, 1500
Average = 343 ms (looks fine)
Median (p50) = 135 ms
p90 about 900 ms -> 1 in 10 users waits nearly a second or more
Target thinking: "p95 < 500 ms" protects the unlucky users.

    🧮 Intermediate: Worked Capacity Estimate

    textFeature: notifications service
DAU 500,000; 12 notifications/user/day = 6,000,000/day
Average = 6,000,000 / 86,400 = 69 per second
Peak factor 8x = 555 per second
Headroom +40% = 777 per second target capacity
Storage: 6M x 0.5 KB = 3 GB/day; 90-day retention = 270 GB
Cost check: 270 GB at 0.10 per GB-month = 27 per month storage

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Technical Product Manager interviews about technical discovery and NFRs, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.What are non-functional requirements?
    Quality attributes such as performance, scalability, availability, security, privacy and maintainability, written as measurable targets and verified through tests and monitoring.
    IQ2.How do you scope NFRs for a new feature?
    Interview stakeholders, understand user journeys and risks, write scenarios with numbers, estimate cost with engineers, agree trade-offs and sign off.
    IQ3.Why use percentiles for latency?
    Averages hide outliers. Percentiles like p95 and p99 show what slower users experience.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.Explain SLI, SLO, SLA and error budget.
    SLI is the measured indicator, SLO the internal target, SLA the customer contract with remedies, and the error budget the allowed unreliability that balances feature work and stability.
    IQ5.What are RTO and RPO?
    Recovery time objective is how fast service must be restored. Recovery point objective is how much data loss is acceptable.
    IQ6.How do you estimate capacity?
    Convert users to requests via actions per user, apply a peak factor and headroom, add data volume and retention, and validate with a load test.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.How do you approach security requirements?
    Threat model with STRIDE, define authentication, authorization, encryption, logging and dependency controls, and verify with testing and reviews.
    IQ8.How do you handle compliance like GDPR or SOC 2?
    Identify applicability, perform a gap assessment, prioritize controls, collect evidence, automate where possible and plan audits.
    IQ9.How do you balance NFRs against cost?
    Present options with cost and risk, link targets to business impact, stage the targets, and use error budgets and triggers to revisit.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.How do you verify NFRs?
    Load, stress and soak tests, security tests, restore drills, synthetic monitoring, SLO dashboards and release gates.
    IQ11.What would you do about a latency regression?
    Confirm with data, roll back or flag off, trace the change, fix, add performance tests to CI and review budgets.
    IQ12.Tell me about a time NFRs changed a product decision.
    Describe the unclear requirement, how you quantified it, the trade-off options, the decision and the measurable outcome.
