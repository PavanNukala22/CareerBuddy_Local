    Module 01

    Tooling & Analytics for Growth Product Managers

    Attribution modeling, marketing tech stack integration (Segment, Braze, AppsFlyer) and behavioral cohorting: the measurement and activation layer that lets a growth team see what works and act on it.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Tooling and analytics give a Growth PM trustworthy eyes and hands. The job is to design the measurement system, connect the tools, and make sure every budget decision is backed by data that can be believed.

    🧭
    Attribution
    Credit channels and prove incrementality.
    🔌
    Tech stack
    Segment, Braze, AppsFlyer integrated.
    👥
    Cohorts
    Behavioral segments and retention curves.
    🛡️
    Trust
    Data quality, identity and privacy.
    💡
    Tools do not create insight. A clean event plan, resolved identities and honest attribution do. The stack serves the questions.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Compare attribution models and explain their biases.
    2. Use incrementality tests and MMM to validate attribution.
    3. Design a customer data stack with Segment, Braze and AppsFlyer roles.
    4. Build a tracking plan and identity resolution approach.
    5. Define behavioral cohorts and read retention curves.
    6. Handle consent, ATT and SKAdNetwork limits responsibly.
    7. Monitor data quality and govern the stack.

    Attribution Modeling

    Single and multi-touch models, data-driven attribution, view-through, incrementality tests and marketing mix modeling.

    🧭 Basic: Attribution Models

    Figure 2.1 · A four-touch customer journey
    ModelPaid socialEmailOrganicPaid searchRule
    Last click$0$0$0$100All to last touch
    First click$100$0$0$0All to first touch
    Linear$25$25$25$25Equal split
    Position-based 40/20/40$40$10$10$4040% first, 40% last, 20% split middle
    📌
    Same journey, four answers. No model is "true"; each one is a choice that favors some channels. Last click overvalues bottom-funnel channels, first click overvalues awareness.
    🎯
    Last click
    Favors closers.
    🔭
    First click
    Favors openers.
    ⚖️
    Multi-touch
    Shares credit.
    🧪
    Incrementality
    Proves causation.

    📊 Intermediate: Data-Driven and View-Through

    ApproachHow it worksCaution
    Data-driven attributionAlgorithm estimates each touch's marginal contributionBlack box; needs volume; platform-specific
    View-throughCredit for ad impressions without clicksInflated by users who would convert anyway
    Platform self-reportingEach ad network claims its conversionsSums exceed 100% of real conversions
    MTA with user-level dataPath-level creditWeakened by privacy limits

    🧪 Advanced: Incrementality Testing and MMM

    textGEO / HOLDOUT INCREMENTALITY TEST
Test group (ads shown): 100,000 users -> 2,300 conversions (2.30%)
Holdout (no ads):       100,000 users -> 2,000 conversions (2.00%)
Incremental conversions = 2,300 - 2,000 = 300 (13% of reported conversions)
Spend = $30,000
Reported CPA = 30,000 / 2,300 = $13.04
Incremental CPA = 30,000 / 300 = $100.00
-> 87% of attributed conversions would have happened anyway
    MethodBest forLimits
    Holdout / ghost adsSingle channel or campaignNeeds traffic; platform support
    Geo experimentsTV, OOH, broad channelsRegion matching; noise
    Marketing mix modeling (MMM)Top-down budget across channelsNeeds long history; coarse; assumptions
    Conversion lift studiesPlatform-run testsPlatform trust; limited visibility

    Real-World Scenarios & Troubleshooting

    Case studies: tools that disagree, an install attribution drop after iOS changes, a Braze campaign with unclear impact and a broken event after a release.

    ⚖️ Case Study: Three Tools, Three Numbers

    Meta reports 1,200 conversions, Google reports 1,100, backend shows 1,500 orders in total.

    Figure 8.1 · Reconciling tool disagreement
    ✅
    Outcome: platforms double-counted overlapping conversions (1,200 + 1,100 = 2,300 claimed vs 1,500 actual). Budget decisions moved to backend-based incrementality tests.

    📱 Case Study: iOS Attribution Drop

    1. Install attribution fell sharply for iOS after ATT.
    2. Check opt-in rate and SKAN postbacks.
    3. Redesign the SKAN conversion value schema.
    4. Use MMM and geo tests for campaign-level decisions.
    Figure 8.2 · Handling an iOS attribution drop

    💬 Troubleshooting: A Campaign With Unclear Impact

    StepAction
    1Check holdout or control group existence
    2Compare conversions of messaged vs holdout
    3Check overlap with other campaigns
    4Compute incremental lift and cost
    5Decide to scale, change or stop

    🐞 Scenario: An Event Broke After a Release

    Signup Completed events dropped 40% after release. Check schema validation errors, compare with backend signups, find the changed SDK call, hot fix, backfill from server logs, and add a release checklist item and volume alert.

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Growth Product Manager interviews about tooling and analytics, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.Compare attribution models.
    Last click favors closers, first click favors openers, linear shares equally, position-based emphasizes ends; data-driven estimates marginal contribution. None proves causation.
    IQ2.How do you prove a channel works?
    Incrementality tests: holdouts or geo experiments to measure conversions caused, then compute incremental CPA, supported by MMM for top-down checks.
    IQ3.What roles do Segment, AppsFlyer and Braze play?
    Segment collects and routes events; AppsFlyer attributes mobile installs and events; Braze runs lifecycle messaging; the warehouse is the source of truth.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.How do you design a tracking plan?
    Start with questions, define object-action events with typed properties, owners and destinations, validate schemas, QA and monitor volumes.
    IQ5.How do you handle identity resolution?
    Link anonymous IDs to authenticated user IDs on identify, use user_id cross-device, avoid false merges and test stitching rules.
    IQ6.How did ATT change attribution?
    Fewer users opt in to tracking, so user-level attribution weakens; use SKAN with a designed conversion value schema, aggregated reporting, MMM and first-party data.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.What is a behavioral cohort?
    A group defined by actions, such as inviting a teammate in week one; compare retention to find activation behaviors and then test causality.
    IQ8.Why do platform reports disagree?
    Different attribution windows, models, view-through credit and double counting; reconcile to backend data.
    IQ9.How do you use Braze for activation?
    Segment by behavior, build canvases with triggers, cap frequency, include control groups and measure incremental impact.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.Client-side vs server-side tracking?
    Server-side is more reliable and accurate for revenue; client-side is easier but lossy; use both with deduplication.
    IQ11.How do you keep data quality high?
    Schema validation, volume alerts, reconciliation with finance and backend, ownership and release checklists.
    IQ12.Tell me about a measurement problem you solved.
    Describe the discrepancy, diagnosis, fix, impact on decisions and the monitoring you added.
