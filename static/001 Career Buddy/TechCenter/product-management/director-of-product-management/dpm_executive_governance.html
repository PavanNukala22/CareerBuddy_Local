    Module 01

    Executive Governance for Directors of Product Management

    Board reporting, cross-departmental alignment with Sales, Finance, Legal and Engineering, and M&A product evaluation: operating at the executive table with rigor and influence.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Executive governance is where product leadership meets corporate leadership. You report honestly to the board, align the functions that make or break plans, and evaluate big moves like acquisitions with a product lens.

    📑
    Board reporting
    Clear, honest, decision-ready.
    🤝
    Alignment
    Sales, Finance, Legal, Engineering.
    🔎
    M&A evaluation
    Product due diligence.
    🏛️
    Governance
    Decision rights and cadence.
    💡
    Executive governance is the discipline of making good decisions together, on time, with clear accountability.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Prepare board and executive reporting that drives decisions.
    2. Align product with Sales, Finance, Legal and Engineering on plans and commitments.
    3. Evaluate acquisition targets from a product and integration perspective.
    4. Design governance: decision rights, operating cadence and planning cycles.
    5. Manage product risk, compliance and legal exposure.
    6. Influence executives with narrative, evidence and trust.
    7. Handle conflicts and escalations at the leadership level.

    📈 Intermediate: KPIs Boards Care About

    KPIDefinitionWhy it matters
    ARRAnnual recurring revenueScale
    Net revenue retention (NRR)(Start ARR + expansion - contraction - churn) / start ARRGrowth from existing customers
    Gross retention(Start ARR - churn - contraction) / start ARRStickiness
    New ARR / pipelineAdded in the periodGrowth engine
    Rule of 40Growth % + profit margin %Efficiency
    Product adoptionActive use of key featuresLeading indicator
    Delivery predictabilityRoadmap items delivered / plannedExecution
    textNRR EXAMPLE
Start ARR $100M; expansion $12M; contraction $2M; churn $6M
NRR = (100 + 12 - 2 - 6) / 100 = 104%
Gross retention = (100 - 2 - 6) / 100 = 92%
Rule of 40: growth 25% + margin 10% = 35 (below 40: watch efficiency)

    💰 Intermediate: Valuation and Synergy Lens

    textDEAL EXAMPLE
Target ARR $15M growing 40%; price $90M = 6.0x ARR. Our own multiple: 5x.
Standalone value at 5x = $75M -> premium paid = $15M.
Synergies:
  Revenue: cross-sell 1,000 shared-profile customers x 10% attach x $20k ACV = $2.0M ARR; value at 5x = $10M
  Cost: $1.5M/year savings; capitalized at 8x = $12M
  Total synergy value = $22M
Integration cost (one-time) = $4M
Net synergy value = $22M - $4M = $18M vs premium $15M -> +$3M. Thin margin: discount synergy risk.
    LensUse
    Revenue multipleMarket comparisons
    DCFCash flow based value
    Strategic valueCapability, speed, defensibility
    Build vs buyCost and time of building internally
    Walk-away priceDiscipline

    🔍 Case Study: Acquisition With Hidden Churn

    1. Cohort-level retention shows 30% annual churn masked by new sales.
    2. Re-value the target with realistic churn.
    3. Negotiate price or structure (earn-out).
    4. Plan retention fixes for integration.

    ⚖️ Troubleshooting: Legal Blocks a Feature a Week Before Launch

    StepAction
    1Understand the exact risk and requirement
    2Explore compliant alternatives (scope, consent, region)
    3Phase rollout to unaffected markets
    4Escalate with options and risk acceptance if needed
    5Add earlier legal gates for future work

    💼 Scenario: Sales Demands a Custom Feature for a $2M Deal

    Assess strategic fit, reusability across customers and cost, offer a configurable path or paid services, set the commercial terms, and escalate to the exec team using decision rights if needed.

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Director of Product interviews about executive governance, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.What goes into a board product update?
    Executive summary, KPIs vs targets, highlights, risks with mitigation and decisions requested, with a pre-read.
    IQ2.How do you communicate bad news to the board?
    Early, factual, cause not blame, plan with owners and dates, and a clear ask.
    IQ3.Which metrics matter to boards?
    ARR, NRR, gross retention, growth, rule of 40, adoption and delivery predictability.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.How do you align with Sales?
    Share roadmap themes with confidence levels, run deal desk, use win/loss and joint planning.
    IQ5.How do you work with Finance?
    Investment cases, budget variance reporting, forecasting ranges and clear ROI methods.
    IQ6.How do you work with Legal?
    Early privacy and IP reviews, compliance by design and clear gates.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.How do you work with Engineering leadership?
    Joint capacity and priorities, fixed allocation for tech debt, architecture participation.
    IQ8.How do you evaluate an acquisition product-wise?
    Thesis and fit, product and tech diligence, customers, valuation and synergies, integration plan.
    IQ9.What red flags in diligence?
    Customer concentration, hidden churn, key person risk, IP issues, tech debt and inflated pipeline.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.How do you set up governance?
    Decision rights, cadence, escalation paths and decision logs.
    IQ11.How do you manage product risk?
    Risk register with scoring, owners, mitigations and review cadence.
    IQ12.Tell me about influencing an executive decision.
    Describe stakeholders, narrative, evidence, pre-wiring and result.
