    Module 01

    People Leadership for Directors of Product Management

    Hiring, building product career ladders, performance management and coaching PM leads and managers: growing a product organization of strong, motivated people.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    People leadership is where a Director earns scale. You hire well, define growth paths, run fair performance systems and develop leaders who lead others.

    💡

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Design structured hiring processes with scorecards and calibrated interviews.
    2. Build product career ladders and a competency framework.

    4. Coach PM leads and managers with frameworks and delegation.
    5. Manage underperformance and difficult conversations.
    6. Measure team health, retention and engagement.
    7. Plan succession and build a strong product culture.

    ⚖️ Intermediate: Calibration

    textCALIBRATION SESSION (24 PMs across 4 teams)
Proposed ratings: Exceeds 4 (17%), Meets 16 (67%), Below 4 (17%)
Check: team A rated 5 of 6 as Exceeds; peers question evidence. After review: 2 stay Exceeds.
Final distribution: Exceeds 3 (13%), Meets 18 (75%), Below 3 (13%)
Rule: discuss evidence against the ladder, not personalities; document rationale.
    Calibration principlePractice
    Evidence firstOutcomes and behaviors vs the ladder
    Compare across teamsConsistent standards
    Challenge outliersOver- and under-rating
    Guard against biasRecency, halo, affinity
    Document decisionsFair and auditable
    Figure 4.2 · Layers of calibration

    🆘 Intermediate: Managing Underperformance

    StepAction
    1 DiagnoseSkill, will, role fit, context, or manager issue?
    2 Direct feedbackSpecific examples; expectations; impact
    3 SupportCoaching, resources, clearer goals
    4 Written planPerformance improvement plan (PIP) with measurable goals and timeline (30 to 60 days)
    5 Check-insWeekly progress reviews
    6 OutcomeSuccess, extend, move role, or exit per HR process
    textPIP EXAMPLE (45 days)
Expectation: deliver quarterly roadmap with metrics and stakeholder sign-off.
Evidence of gap: two missed deadlines; unclear success metrics in 3 specs.
Goals: (1) spec template with metrics for next 2 features by day 21; (2) stakeholder review before kickoff; (3) roadmap approved by day 45.
Support: weekly coaching; senior PM buddy; spec review.
Check-ins: weekly. Involve HR from start.
    ⚠️
    Involve HR early, document facts not opinions, and treat the person with dignity. A PIP is a real chance to improve, not a paperwork step to exit someone.

    Real-World Scenarios & Troubleshooting

    Case studies: losing a top performer, a toxic high performer, a stalled promotion and a PM lead who micromanages.

    🚪 Case Study: A Top Performer Resigns

    Your strongest senior PM announces she is leaving for a bigger scope elsewhere.

    Figure 8.1 · Responding to resignation
    ✅
    Outcome: the root cause was no visible path to Principal. The Director created clear principal criteria and two stretch roles, which reduced regretted attrition from 20% to 8% in a year.

    ⚡ Case Study: The Toxic High Performer

    1. Document behaviors and impact with specifics.
    2. Give direct feedback and expectations on how results are achieved.
    3. Involve HR; set a time-bound plan.
    4. Be willing to part ways if behavior does not change.
    Figure 8.2 · Handling a toxic high performer

    🛑 Troubleshooting: Stalled Promotion

    StepAction
    1Compare evidence to the next-level criteria
    2Identify gaps in scope, impact or behaviors
    3Agree on a specific plan and timeline
    4Create stretch opportunities
    5Revisit in the next cycle with evidence

    🔬 Scenario: A PM Lead Micromanages

    Use GROW and feedback: show impact (team morale, slower decisions), clarify decision rights, practice delegation levels, coach on trust and outcomes over tasks, and follow up with team feedback.

    IQ1.How do you structure PM hiring?
    Define role and competencies, source diverse pipelines, structured interviews with scorecards, evidence-based debrief and fast closing.
    IQ2.How do you reduce bias in hiring?
    Structured questions, independent feedback, diverse panels, calibration and tracking pipeline diversity.
    IQ3.How do you build a career ladder?
    Define levels by scope and impact, competencies with examples, dual track, calibration and governance.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.How do you decide promotions?
    Evidence of sustained next-level performance via written case, peer input and calibration.
    IQ5.How do you run calibration?
    Compare evidence to the ladder across teams, challenge outliers, guard bias and document.
    IQ6.How do you handle underperformance?
    Diagnose, give direct feedback, support, write a PIP with measurable goals and involve HR.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.How do you coach PM leads?
    GROW, regular 1:1s, delegation levels and feedback; develop judgment, not dependence.
    IQ8.How do you transition from PM to Director?
    Shift from doing to enabling through leads, systems and strategy.
    IQ9.How do you measure team health?
    Attrition, eNPS, workload, career movement and manager effectiveness.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.How do you retain top talent?
    Growth paths, impact, good managers, autonomy, recognition and fair pay.
    IQ11.How do you build culture?
    Model values, rituals, learning, mentoring, inclusion and onboarding.
    IQ12.Tell me about a hard people decision.
    Describe context, options, decision, communication and outcome.
