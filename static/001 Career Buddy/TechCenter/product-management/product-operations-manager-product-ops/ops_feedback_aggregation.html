    Module 01

    Data & Feedback Aggregation for Product Operations

    Centralizing qualitative customer feedback from sales, support and customer success, and turning it into synthesized, prioritized insights that product managers can act on.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Data and feedback aggregation turns scattered anecdotes into a trusted evidence base. Product Ops builds the pipeline from sales, support and success into prioritized insights.

    📥
    Collect
    Feedback from every channel.
    🏷️
    Tag
    Taxonomy and enrichment.
    🧠
    Synthesize
    Themes and insights.
    🔄
    Close loop
    Tell customers what happened.
    💡
    Feedback is only useful when it is centralized, structured and weighted. Loud anecdotes are not evidence.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Map feedback sources from sales, support and customer success.
    2. Build a feedback intake system and taxonomy.
    3. Enrich feedback with account, segment and revenue data.
    4. Synthesize themes with qualitative methods and quantification.
    5. Deliver insight reports and briefings that PMs use.
    6. Close the loop with customers and internal teams.
    7. Measure feedback program quality and impact.

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Product Operations interviews about feedback and insights, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.How do you centralize customer feedback?
    Map sources, integrate CRM, support and call tools into one hub with required fields and a triage SLA.
    IQ2.How do you design a feedback taxonomy?
    Small, stable, mutually exclusive tags by area, theme, type and segment, tested with sample tagging and reviewed quarterly.
    IQ3.How do you use AI in tagging?
    AI proposes tags, humans review and audit accuracy; refine with examples; keep monitoring.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.How do you prioritize feedback themes?
    Count, ARR weighting, segment fit, severity and strategy; present several views.
    IQ5.What makes a good insight?
    States who, what and why, with evidence, implication and confidence.
    IQ6.How do you handle a loud customer?
    Check breadth and revenue, cap outliers, understand the underlying problem and communicate options.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.How do you reduce bias in feedback?
    Combine sources, use full-period counts, add usage data, probe to problems, look for disconfirming evidence.
    IQ8.How do you deliver insights to PMs?
    Weekly digest, monthly report, dashboards and deep dives linked to Productboard.
    IQ9.How do you close the loop?
    Notify requesters and field teams of outcomes, automate with status changes, track closure rate.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.How do you get field teams to contribute?
    Make intake easy, close loops, share roadmap, recognize contributors.
    IQ11.How do you measure the program?
    Coverage, tag accuracy, time to synthesis, PM usage, closure rate and decisions citing insights.
    IQ12.Tell me about an insight that changed a decision.
    Describe feedback, synthesis, evidence, recommendation and outcome.
