    Module 01

    Launch Readiness for Product Operations

    Standardizing go-to-market enablement checklists, release notes management and internal training programs so every launch lands smoothly across product, marketing, sales, support and success.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Launch readiness is the discipline that makes releases boring in the best way. Product Ops standardizes checklists, communication and training so every team launches with confidence.

    ✅
    Checklists
    Standard launch readiness gates.
    📝
    Release notes
    Clear, consistent communication.
    🎓
    Enablement
    Training for field teams.
    🛰️
    Launch ops
    Tiers, calendars and reviews.
    💡
    A launch is not done when code ships. It is done when customers and internal teams are ready, informed and supported.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Define launch tiers and standard readiness checklists.
    2. Coordinate go-to-market across product, marketing, sales and support.
    3. Manage release notes and change communication at scale.
    4. Design enablement and internal training programs.
    5. Run launch calendars, readiness reviews and go/no-go decisions.
    6. Measure launch quality and post-launch outcomes.
    7. Handle launch risks, rollbacks and incident communication.

    📝 Release Notes Standards

    ElementStandard
    AudienceCustomers (external); internal teams (internal notes)
    StructureNew, Improved, Fixed, Deprecated; most important first
    VoiceBenefit-led, plain language, no jargon
    DetailWhat changed, who it affects, how to use, link to help
    VisualsScreenshots or short GIFs for key items
    CadencePer release plus monthly roundup
    OwnershipProduct Ops runs process; PM writes draft; PMM or docs edits
    textRELEASE NOTE EXAMPLE
New: Scheduled report exports
You can now schedule weekly exports of any report to Excel or CSV and email them to your team.
Who: Admins and report owners on Pro and Enterprise plans
How: Open a report > Schedule > choose frequency and recipients. Learn more: [help article]
Available: October 14 for all customers

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Product Operations interviews about launch readiness, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.How do you standardize launches?
    Define tiers, cross-functional checklists, readiness reviews, owners and a launch calendar scaled to impact.
    IQ2.What is in a launch checklist?
    Product, engineering, marketing, sales, support, CS, legal and docs items with owners and gates.
    IQ3.How do you run go/no-go?
    Review readiness scores and risks a week before launch with clear criteria and decision makers.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.How do you manage release notes?
    Standards, benefit-led drafts from PMs, editing by PMM/docs, approval, publishing and notifications with metrics.
    IQ5.How do you communicate breaking changes?
    Advance notice, targeted comms, migration guides, sunset schedule and support readiness.
    IQ6.How do you design enablement?
    Audience needs, short outcome-based training, practice, assessment, central hub and refresh cycles.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.How do you measure enablement?
    Completion, quiz scores, behavior (usage in pitches) and business impact like deals and tickets.
    IQ8.How do you handle a slipping launch?
    Assess dependencies, choose cut or delay, decide with leaders, update calendar and inform teams.
    IQ9.How do you reduce launch risk?
    Feature flags, staged rollout, pre-set rollback criteria, incident comms templates and hypercare.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.How do you run hypercare?
    Dashboard, daily standups, issue triage, exit criteria and adoption tracking.
    IQ11.How do you measure launch success?
    Checklist completion, timeliness, adoption, CSAT, support load and business outcomes at 30/60/90 days.
    IQ12.Tell me about a launch you improved.
    Describe the problem, process change, results and learning.
