    Module 01

    Foundations of Enterprise Governance

    Why large clients need security, compliance and procurement approval, and how the TAM helps them get there without stalling the deal or the project.

    🚀 Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Enterprise clients cannot simply start using a product. They must pass security review, prove compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR) and clear procurement. The TAM navigates these hurdles by knowing the answers, finding the evidence and keeping every approver informed.

    🔐
    Security
    Questionnaires, evidence, pen-test reports.
    📜
    Compliance
    SOC 2, HIPAA and GDPR in plain terms.
    🧾
    Procurement
    Vendor onboarding, contracts, approvals.
    🤝
    Stakeholders
    Security, legal, privacy, finance, IT.
    🏠
    Analogy: governance is airport security. Everyone must pass, the rules are non-negotiable, and a prepared traveller with the right documents moves through fastest.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Run a client security review from intake to approval.
    2. Answer security questionnaires accurately and consistently.
    3. Explain SOC 2, HIPAA and GDPR and what each requires from a vendor.
    4. Handle data privacy, residency and breach-notification questions.
    5. Move a deal through procurement and vendor onboarding.
    6. Read contract terms (MSA, DPA, BAA, SLA) well enough to escalate correctly.

    Module 03

    Compliance Frameworks: SOC 2, HIPAA & GDPR

    What each framework requires of a vendor, and how to explain them to clients.

    📜 Basic: The Three Frameworks

    FrameworkApplies toCore requirementProof
    SOC 2Service organisations (global)Controls for security, availability, confidentiality…Auditor report (Type I / II)
    HIPAAUS health data (PHI)Administrative, physical, technical safeguardsBAA + internal controls
    GDPRPersonal data of people in the EULawful basis, rights, security, breach noticeDPA, records, transfer mechanism
    📌
    SOC 2 is an audit. HIPAA and GDPR are laws. A vendor is not "HIPAA certified" or "GDPR certified" by default; it complies.

    🧾 Intermediate: SOC 2 Type I vs Type II

    TypeTestsPeriodClient trust
    Type IDesign of controlsA single point in timeLower
    Type IIDesign and operating effectiveness3 to 12 monthsHigher; usually required

    The five Trust Services Criteria are Security (always included), Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality and Privacy.

    🏥 Intermediate: HIPAA in Practice

    • PHI = health information that identifies a person.
    • A vendor handling PHI is a business associate and must sign a BAA.
    • Safeguards: encryption, access control, audit logs, workforce training.
    • Breach notification to affected parties without unreasonable delay and within 60 days.

    🇪🇺 Advanced: GDPR Roles & Rights

    ConceptMeaningTAM action
    ControllerDecides why and how data is used (usually the client)Respect their instructions
    ProcessorProcesses data for the controller (usually you)Sign a DPA
    Data subject rightsAccess, erasure, portability, correctionKnow the request process; typical reply within 1 month
    Breach noticeAuthority notified within 72 hoursEscalate immediately
    FinesUp to €20M or 4% of global turnoverExplain why the client cares

    Moving EU personal data outside the EU needs a legal mechanism such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or an adequacy decision. Offering regional hosting often removes the issue.

    Module 06

    Contracts & Legal Terms

    Read the key agreements well enough to answer practical questions and escalate correctly. The TAM is not a lawyer.

    📑 Intermediate: The Contract Stack

    DocumentPurposeWho owns it
    MSAMaster terms for the relationshipLegal / sales
    Order formWhat is bought, price, termSales
    DPARules for processing personal dataLegal / privacy
    BAARules for handling PHILegal / compliance
    SLAAvailability and support commitmentsLegal / support

    📉 Intermediate: SLA Credit Math

    textMonthly fee $10,000. SLA 99.9%. Credits: below 99.9% = 10%, below 99.0% = 25%.
Outage 90 min in 43,200 min → availability = (43,200 - 90) / 43,200 = 99.79%
99.79% < 99.9% → credit = 10% x $10,000 = $1,000

    ⚖️ Advanced: Negotiation Guardrails

    • Never promise a custom legal term; route it to legal.
    • Know common asks: liability caps, audit rights, termination for convenience, indemnity.
    • Separate technical truth (what we do) from legal promise (what we commit to).
    • Document every client exception request and its outcome.

    Module 08

    Scenarios & Troubleshooting

    Practise judgement on realistic governance situations.

    🏥 Case Study: The Hospital Client

    A hospital wants to use the product for appointment reminders that include patient names. They ask if you are "HIPAA compliant".

    • Clarify: PHI is involved, so a BAA is required.
    • Share the HIPAA safeguards summary, not the word "certified".
    • Escalate the BAA to legal; do not start production use until signed.

    🇩🇪 Case Study: The EU Data Residency Demand

    A German client requires all data to stay in the EU. Your default region is US.

    🎯
    Offer an EU region, update the data map and sub-processor list, confirm backups and logs also stay in region, and record this in the DPA.

    🚨 Case Study: A Breach Notice

    A client's admin credentials were exposed. Their contract requires notice within 48 hours.

    1. Escalate to security and legal immediately.
    2. Confirm facts, scope and affected data.
    3. Send a first notice inside the clock with what is known and when the next update comes.

    ⏳ Scenario: Review Stuck for 6 Weeks

    CauseAction
    No answer from client securityEscalate to sponsor with dates
    Unknown questionsHold a live walkthrough call
    Missing SOC 2 bridge letterProvide a bridge letter covering the gap period

    Module 11

    Interview Prep

    Eight interview questions across four levels. Say your answer out loud first, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.What is SOC 2?
    An audit report on a vendor's security controls; Type II proves they operated effectively over time.
    IQ2.What is a BAA?
    An agreement required under HIPAA when a vendor handles PHI for a covered entity.

    🌿 Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ3.How do you answer a 200-question security questionnaire?
    Reuse the approved answer library, route new items to security, add evidence references and agree a return date.
    IQ4.Controller vs processor?
    Controller decides purpose and means; processor handles data on its behalf under a DPA.

    🌳 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ5.A client demands EU-only data. What do you do?
    Map data flows, offer EU hosting or SCCs, update DPA and sub-processor list, confirm backups and logs.
    IQ6.How do you plan around slow procurement?
    Plan backwards from go-live, run reviews in parallel, ask early for forms and approvers, add buffer.

    🏔️ Expert Interview Questions

    IQ7.Describe a deal you unblocked.
    Tell a story: blocker, who you engaged, evidence you produced, outcome and lessons.
    IQ8.How do you scale governance across many accounts?
    Standard evidence packs, answer library, calendar of renewals and audits, tiered review depth, regular reporting.
