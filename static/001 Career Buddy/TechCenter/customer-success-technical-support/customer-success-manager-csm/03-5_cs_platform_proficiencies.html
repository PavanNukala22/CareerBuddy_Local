    Module 01 · What Is What

    What a Customer Success Platform Is

    Start from the basics: what a CS platform does, why CS teams use one, the types of tools in a CS tech stack, and the core capabilities every platform shares.

    🧰 What Is a CS Platform?

    A Customer Success platform (also called CSP or CS software) is a system that brings together data about each customer (contracts, product usage, support tickets, surveys, conversations) and helps CSMs monitor health, prioritise work and automate engagement.

    Figure 1.1 · Data flows into a CS platform; actions flow out

    🎯 Why CS Teams Use One

    👁️
    One view
    Everything about an account in one place (Customer 360).
    🚦
    Early warning
    Health scores and alerts flag risk before renewal.
    📋
    Consistent process
    Playbooks guide every CSM through the same steps.
    ⚙️
    Scale
    Automated emails and journeys cover many accounts.
    📊
    Reporting
    Leaders see retention, health and team activity.

    Gainsight

    The most widely known enterprise CS platform. Learn Customer 360, Scorecards, Cockpit and CTAs, Playbooks, Success Plans, Timeline, Journey Orchestrator, Rules Engine and reporting.

    🟣 Gainsight at a Glance

    AreaUse
    Gainsight CSCore CS platform (C360, scorecards, cockpit, success plans)
    Gainsight PXProduct experience: in-app analytics and guides
    Customer CommunitiesCommunity forums
    Staircase AI (acquired)Relationship and sentiment insights from communication data
    DeploymentSalesforce-integrated or standalone (Gainsight NXT)

    👁️ Customer 360 (C360)

    • One page per customer: summary (ARR, renewal date, CSM, segment), scorecard, contacts/people, usage, CTAs, success plans, timeline, surveys.
    • Admins configure which sections and fields appear.
    • Use it to prepare for every call: read timeline and open CTAs first.

    📊 Scorecards

    • A scorecard is made of measures (e.g., Product Usage, Support, NPS, Engagement) with weights.
    • Measures can be updated automatically (by rules) or manually (e.g., CSM sentiment).
    • Overall score shows as a number/grade and colour; history shows trends.
    • Different scorecards can apply to different segments.

    📋 Cockpit, CTAs & Playbooks

    ElementMeaning
    CockpitThe CSM's daily work list of CTAs
    CTA (Call to Action)A work item: Risk, Opportunity, Lifecycle, Activity types
    PlaybookSet of tasks attached to a CTA (e.g., "Risk – usage drop")
    Priority / due dateOrder your day
    Closing a CTARecord reason/outcome for reporting
    CTA exampleType: Risk · Reason: Usage Drop · Priority: High · Due: 10 Oct
Playbook: "Usage Recovery"
  ☐ Review usage by module
  ☐ Call champion
  ☐ Agree recovery actions
  ☐ Log success-plan objective

    🗺️ Success Plans & Timeline

    🎯 Success Plans

    Objectives and tasks shared with the customer (can be exported/shared). Track goals discovered at kickoff and reviewed at QBRs.

    🕰️ Timeline

    Activity log: meetings, calls, emails, notes, updates. Can sync with email/calendar. The memory of the account.

    ⚙️ Journey Orchestrator & Rules Engine

    FeatureWhat it doesExample
    Journey Orchestrator (JO)Automated, multi-step email programmes; surveysOnboarding emails; NPS surveys
    Rules EngineScheduled rules that load/transform data, update scores, create CTAsCreate risk CTA when usage drops 30%
    Reports & DashboardsBuild charts on any objectRenewals by health; CTA completion by CSM
    Data Designer / Data ManagementCombine and prepare data setsJoin usage and contract data
    📌
    Rules and JO are usually built by CS Ops/admins. CSMs should know they exist, what triggers their CTAs, and how to request changes.

    🏋️ Practice & Quick Check

    Practice
    P1.Describe how you'd start your day in Gainsight.
    Open Cockpit and sort CTAs by priority/due date; handle overdue risk CTAs first; check scorecard changes on key accounts; review C360 and timeline before today's meetings; log notes after each call; update success-plan tasks.
    Practice
    P2.Which Gainsight features would you use to automate an onboarding email series and create a CSM task if setup isn't complete by day 14?

    Scenario: Scaling onboarding.

    Journey Orchestrator for the email series; Rules Engine (or JO logic) to check the setup milestone at day 14 and create a Lifecycle/Risk CTA with an onboarding playbook in the CSM's Cockpit.

    ChurnZero

    A CS platform known for real-time usage tracking and automation. Learn the account view, ChurnScore, Segments, Plays, Journeys, in-app WalkThroughs and surveys.

    🟠 ChurnZero at a Glance

    • Tracks real-time product usage via a JavaScript snippet or API events.
    • Strong automation and in-app communication.
    • Integrates with CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot) and support tools.
    • Popular with mid-market SaaS teams.

    👁️ Account View & Command Center

    • Account overview: ARR, renewal, ChurnScore, contacts, usage trends, tasks, notes.
    • Command Center: configurable home dashboards for CSMs and leaders.
    • Usage views show which features and users are active.

    ❤️ ChurnScore

    • ChurnZero's health score; built from weighted factors (usage, logins, support, NPS, billing…).
    • Admins configure factors and weights; different models can apply to different segments.
    • Trend and factor breakdown show why a score changed.

    🧩 Segments & Plays

    FeatureUseExample
    SegmentsDynamic groups of accounts/contacts by criteria"Enterprise, renewal < 120 days, ChurnScore < 50"
    PlaysTask sequences triggered by events or segment entry"Low usage play": review → call → plan
    TasksIndividual work items for CSMsCall champion by Friday

    ✉️ Journeys, WalkThroughs & Surveys

    FeatureWhat it does
    JourneysMap customer stages (e.g., onboarding milestones) and track progress
    Automations / campaignsEmails and tasks triggered by behaviour
    WalkThroughsIn-app guided tours inside your product
    Right Rail / in-app messagesContextual messages and resources inside the app
    SurveysNPS/CSAT in-app or by email, feeding ChurnScore
    💡
    Real-time usage + in-app messages enables "moment of need" help: e.g., show a WalkThrough when a user opens a feature for the first time.

    🚫 Common Mistakes

    MistakeWhy it hurtsDo this instead
    Tracking every eventNoisy dataTrack key events tied to value
    Too many in-app messagesAnnoys usersTarget by segment and behaviour
    Ignoring ChurnScore factorsDon't know what to fixReview factor breakdown

    🏋️ Practice & Quick Check

    Practice
    P1.Define a ChurnZero segment and play for accounts at risk before renewal.
    Segment: renewal date within 120 days AND ChurnScore < 50 AND ARR ≥ threshold. Play: day 1 review usage and tickets; day 3 call champion; day 7 recovery plan with owners; day 14 exec outreach if no improvement; weekly re-check.
    Practice
    P2.How would you use ChurnZero to increase adoption of a reporting feature?

    Scenario: Feature adoption push.

    Track the "report created" event; create a segment of active users who never created a report; launch an in-app WalkThrough and a short email automation for that segment; measure the event count weekly; give CSMs a play for key accounts still not adopting.

    🔗 Identity Matching

    • Every system must identify the same customer and user consistently.
    • Common keys: CRM Account ID, internal tenant/company ID, user email/user ID.
    • Mismatches cause usage to land on the wrong account or none.
    matching exampleCRM Account ID: 0015g00000AbcD  ↔  Product tenant_id: acme-prod  ↔  Zendesk org: Acme Logistics
Mapping table maintained by CS Ops so all three resolve to one customer.

    🧹 Data Hygiene

    PracticeWhy
    Required fields (CSM owner, segment, renewal date)Reports and routing work
    Standard picklists (risk reasons)Consistent analysis
    Log activities promptlyAccurate engagement data
    Remove duplicatesCorrect counts and health
    Regular audits by CS OpsCatch drift early

    🛠️ Troubleshooting Bad Data

    1. Identify the wrong value and where it's displayed.
    2. Find the source system and sync path.
    3. Check mapping/IDs and last sync time and errors.
    4. Compare the raw value in the source system.
    5. Fix at the source; request CS Ops to correct mapping or re-sync.
    💡
    Before telling a customer "your usage is low", sanity-check the data. Wrong data in a QBR destroys credibility.

    Editing synced fields manuallyOverwritten or conflictingFix at the system of record

    Practice
    P2.List four data hygiene rules you'd propose for your CS team.

    Module 08 · Real-World Workflow

    A CSM's Day in the Platform & Building Automation

    How expert CSMs actually use CS platforms: a daily and weekly workflow, building playbooks and journeys, and reporting to leadership.

    ☀️ Daily Workflow

    1. Open your task list (Cockpit/plays/tasks): overdue and high-priority first.
    2. Check alerts: health drops, risk flags, NPS detractors.
    3. Before each meeting: review account view, timeline, open tasks, tickets.
    4. After each meeting: log notes, update success plan, create follow-up tasks.
    5. End of day: clear completed tasks; plan tomorrow.

    📅 Weekly & Monthly Routines

    CadenceActivity
    WeeklyReview book of business by health; renewals ≤120 days; risk updates; pipeline of expansions
    MonthlyUsage trends, success-plan progress, data hygiene check
    QuarterlyQBR prep from platform data; segment review; health model feedback to CS Ops

    📋 Designing a Playbook

    playbook design: "Onboarding (high-touch)"Trigger: Contract signed (CRM opportunity Closed Won, new customer)
Owner: assigned CSM
Steps:
  Day 0   Internal handoff call with AE          (task)
  Day 2   Kickoff meeting; capture objectives    (task + success plan)
  Day 7   SSO/integrations confirmed             (milestone)
  Day 14  First-value check (key event done?)   (auto-check → risk task if not)
  Day 30  Onboarding review; move to Adoption    (lifecycle stage update)
Success criteria: first value ≤ 14 days; go-live ≤ 45 days

    ✉️ Designing a Digital Journey

    tech-touch adoption journeyEntry: new user created (SMB segment)
Day 0   Welcome email + 3-minute setup video
Day 3   If "first project" not created → in-app guide + reminder email
Day 7   Tips email for top 3 features
Day 14  If still inactive → task for pooled CSM team
Day 30  NPS survey → detractors create tasks; promoters invited to review/referral
    💡
    Use branches based on behaviour (did they do X?) instead of sending everyone the same emails.

    📊 Reporting to Leadership

    DashboardShows
    Portfolio healthAccounts and ARR by green/yellow/red
    Renewal forecastUpcoming renewals by forecast category
    Risk registerTop risks, reasons, owners
    AdoptionKey feature adoption by segment

    🛠️ Implementation Basics

    1. Define processes first: segments, lifecycle, health model, key playbooks.
    2. Connect data: CRM, product usage, support, billing; fix IDs.
    3. Configure account views, health, playbooks, dashboards.
    4. Pilot with a few CSMs; gather feedback.
    5. Train the team; set data hygiene rules; iterate.

    🚀 Ramping Up on a New Tool

    • Learn the data model: what objects exist and how they connect.
    • Find the "daily work" screen (task list) and the account view.
    • Understand how health is calculated and what triggers your tasks.
    • Use vendor academies: Gainsight has Gainsight Academy; most vendors offer free training and certifications; Salesforce has Trailhead.
    • Shadow an experienced colleague for a day.

    🎤 Concept Questions

    QuestionModel answer
    Which CS platforms have you used and how?Name one deeply (e.g., Gainsight: C360, scorecards, Cockpit CTAs, playbooks, success plans, JO) and map concepts to others.
    What is a health score in your platform?A weighted combination of measures (usage, support, NPS, engagement, financial) configured by CS Ops; I monitor trends and factors.
    What is a CTA / Play?A work item for the CSM created by a trigger or manually, often with a playbook of steps.
    How do CS platforms get data?Native connectors (CRM, help desk), APIs/JS snippets for usage, warehouse sync, CSV.
    How do you keep data accurate?Log activities promptly, required fields, picklists, fix at source, regular audits with CS Ops.
    How do you use Salesforce as a CSM?Account and contact roles, renewal opportunities, activities, cases for context, reports/dashboards.
    How do you scale with a small team?Segments + automated journeys + behaviour-based triggers, humans for high-value risk.
