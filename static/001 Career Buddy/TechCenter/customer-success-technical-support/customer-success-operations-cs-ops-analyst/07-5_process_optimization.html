    Module 01

    Foundations of CS Process Optimization

    Why smooth handoffs and right-sized capacity matter.

    🏛️ Course Overview

    Process optimisation removes friction between Sales, Customer Success and Support and makes sure CSM teams have the capacity to serve every customer well. You will map processes, standardise handoffs and plan bandwidth.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    1. Map and diagnose a customer process.
    2. Design Sales-to-CS and CS-to-Support handoffs.
    3. Plan CSM capacity and coverage.
    4. Track process metrics.
    5. Lead continuous improvement.

    📖 Core Vocabulary

    TermMeaning
    HandoffTransfer of a customer or task between teams
    SLAAgreed response or delivery time
    CapacityWork a team can handle in a period
    Coverage modelHow accounts are assigned and served
    BottleneckStep that limits overall throughput

    Module 03

    Sales-to-CS Handoff

    Start the customer relationship well.

    🤝 Handoff Package

    • Customer goals and success criteria.
    • Stakeholders and decision makers.
    • Promises and commitments made in the sale.
    • Timeline, risks and contract details.

    📋 Standard Process

    Use a template, an internal handoff call and a kickoff within a set number of days (for example 5 business days).

    📊 Measure It

    MetricWhy
    Time to kickoffSpeed of start
    Time to first valueOnboarding effectiveness
    Repeat questions askedQuality of handoff

    Module 05

    Capacity Planning for CSM Teams

    Right-size workload to protect quality.

    📐 Capacity Formula

    capacity.pyhours = 120  # CSM hours per month for accounts
need = {'enterprise': 12, 'mid': 5, 'smb': 1}
book = {'enterprise': 4, 'mid': 10, 'smb': 20}
used = sum(need[t]*n for t, n in book.items())
print(used, 'of', hours)  # 118 of 120

    🧮 Inputs

    • Available hours minus meetings and admin.
    • Hours per account by tier.
    • Expected renewals and onboarding load.

    🔭 Forecasting

    Use new-business forecasts and churn to plan hiring ahead of demand.

    Module 08

    Scenarios and Troubleshooting

    Resolve real process problems.

    😓 Case: CSMs Overloaded

    1. Calculate hours needed versus available.
    2. Move low-value work to digital CS.
    3. Rebalance accounts and request headcount with data.

    📞 Case: Customers Repeat Themselves

    1. Audit the Sales-to-CS handoff notes.
    2. Introduce a required template.
    3. Measure repeat questions after the change.

    🔀 Troubleshooting

    SymptomCauseFix
    Slow kickoffNo SLA or ownerSet SLA and assign owner
    Tickets bounceUnclear ownershipPublish RACI and escalation rules
    BurnoutCapacity not plannedCapacity model and tiering

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    CS Ops analyst interview questions with model answers.

    🌱 Beginner Questions

    IQ1.Why do handoffs fail?
    Missing context, unclear ownership and no standard process.
    IQ2.What goes in a Sales-to-CS handoff?
    Customer goals, stakeholders, promises made, risks, timeline and contract details.

    🛠️ Intermediate Questions

    IQ3.How do you plan CSM capacity?
    Available hours divided by hours per account by tier, adjusted for onboarding and renewal load.
    IQ4.How do you scale low-touch customers?
    Digital CS: automated emails, in-app guides and webinars, with escalation to a CSM when needed.

    🏆 Advanced Questions

    IQ5.CSMs are overloaded. What do you do?
    Quantify load versus capacity, tier accounts, automate low-value tasks, rebalance and make a data-backed hiring case.
    IQ6.How do you drive process change?
    Baseline metrics, pilot, measure, involve teams, share wins and review regularly.
