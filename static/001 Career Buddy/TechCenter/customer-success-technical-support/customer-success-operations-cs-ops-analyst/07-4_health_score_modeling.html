    Module 01

    Foundations of Health Scoring

    What a health score is and what it must achieve.

    🏛️ Course Overview

    A health score is a single number or colour that summarises how likely a customer is to renew and grow. A good model combines product usage, support tickets, NPS and commercial signals so CSMs can focus on the right accounts at the right time.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    1. Select predictive inputs.
    2. Normalise and weight multiple variables.
    3. Implement the model in SQL or a CS platform.
    4. Validate the score against real churn.
    5. Turn scores into actions.

    📖 Core Vocabulary

    TermMeaning
    Health scoreComposite indicator of renewal likelihood
    Leading indicatorSignal that changes before churn happens
    Lagging indicatorSignal that changes after the damage is done
    NormalisationScaling inputs to a common range such as 0–100
    Back-testingChecking a model against past outcomes

    Module 02

    Choosing Predictive Inputs

    Pick signals that really relate to churn.

    📈 Input Families

    FamilyExamples
    UsageLogins, active users, key-feature use
    SupportTicket volume, severity, time to resolve
    SentimentNPS, CSAT, CSM sentiment
    CommercialPayment delays, contract size, tenure
    EngagementMeeting attendance, training completed

    🔎 Leading vs Lagging

    Prefer leading indicators such as falling weekly active users over lagging ones such as a cancellation request.

    ⚠️ Pitfalls

    • Too many inputs hide the signal.
    • Inputs that are hard to refresh become stale.
    • Avoid inputs that only exist for some customers.

    Module 03

    Normalising and Weighting

    Combine variables into one score.

    📏 Normalise to 0–100

    normalise.sql-- min-max scaling
SELECT account_id,
  100.0*(wau - MIN(wau) OVER()) /
  NULLIF(MAX(wau) OVER() - MIN(wau) OVER(),0) AS usage_score
FROM account_usage;

    ⚖️ Weighted Formula

    score.pydef health(usage, support, nps):
    return 0.5*usage + 0.2*support + 0.3*nps

print(health(80, 60, 70))  # 73.0

    🚦 Thresholds

    Typical bands: 70+ Green, 40–69 Yellow, below 40 Red. Tune on your own data.

    Module 04

    Building the Model

    Implement the score in SQL and the CS platform.

    🧱 Account-Level Table

    inputs.sqlSELECT a.account_id,
  u.usage_score, s.support_score, n.nps_score
FROM accounts a
LEFT JOIN usage_scores u USING (account_id)
LEFT JOIN support_scores s USING (account_id)
LEFT JOIN nps_scores n USING (account_id);

    🎨 Buckets

    bucket.sqlSELECT account_id, score,
  CASE WHEN score >= 70 THEN 'Green'
       WHEN score >= 40 THEN 'Yellow'
       ELSE 'Red' END AS band
FROM scores;

    📉 Trend and Missing Data

    Store score history to show trend. Decide how to treat missing inputs (neutral value or reweight) and document it.

    Module 06

    Validation and Back-testing

    Prove the score predicts churn.

    🧪 Back-testing

    Score past customers as of 90 days before renewal and compare to who churned. Red accounts should churn more than Green.

    📊 Measures

    MeasureMeaning
    PrecisionOf accounts flagged Red, how many churned
    RecallOf churned accounts, how many were flagged Red
    LiftHow much more likely Red churns than average

    🔁 Recalibrate

    Review quarterly, adjust weights and thresholds, and log every change.

    Module 08

    Scenarios and Troubleshooting

    Fix real model problems.

    🟢 Case: Green Accounts Are Churning

    1. Back-test to see which inputs failed to warn.
    2. Add a leading indicator such as seat usage trend.
    3. Re-weight and re-validate.

    🔴 Case: Everything Is Red

    1. Check normalisation and thresholds.
    2. Look for missing data counted as zero.
    3. Recalibrate the bands.

    🔀 Troubleshooting

    SymptomCauseFix
    Scores never changeStale inputsFix refresh jobs
    CSMs ignore scoresNo trustShow drivers and involve CSMs
    Wide score swingsNoisy inputSmooth with rolling average

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    CS Ops analyst interview questions with model answers.

    🌱 Beginner Questions

    IQ1.What is a health score?
    A composite indicator of renewal likelihood using usage, support, sentiment and commercial signals.
    IQ2.Leading vs lagging indicators?
    Leading signals change before churn, such as falling usage; lagging signals appear after, such as cancellation.

    🛠️ Intermediate Questions

    IQ3.How do you choose weights?
    Start from regression or expert judgment, then test on historical churn and refine.
    IQ4.How do you validate the model?
    Back-test on past customers using a 90-day pre-renewal snapshot and measure precision, recall and lift.

    🏆 Advanced Questions

    IQ5.Everything shows as Red. Why?
    Likely normalisation, thresholds or missing data treated as zero; recalibrate and check data quality.
    IQ6.How do you make CSMs trust the score?
    Explain drivers, involve CSMs in design, validate openly and act on it consistently.
