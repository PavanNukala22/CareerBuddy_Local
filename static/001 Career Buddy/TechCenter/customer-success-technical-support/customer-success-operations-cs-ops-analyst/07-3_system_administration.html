    Module 01

    Foundations of the CS Tech Stack

    Know what each system does and how they fit together.

    🏛️ Course Overview

    A CS Ops admin keeps the systems of record and engagement running: Salesforce and HubSpot (CRM), Gainsight and ChurnZero (customer success platforms). You configure objects, fields, workflows and integrations so CSMs see accurate, timely data.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    1. Explain the role of CRM versus CS platform.
    2. Administer objects, fields, roles and permissions.
    3. Configure CTAs, playbooks and health scores.
    4. Design reliable integrations and field mappings.
    5. Automate workflows and govern change safely.

    📖 Core Vocabulary

    TermMeaning
    CRMSystem of record for accounts, contacts and deals
    CS platformTool for health scores, CTAs and success plans
    CTACall to Action: a task prompting a CSM to act
    PlaybookReusable set of tasks for a situation
    SyncScheduled data movement between systems

    Module 03

    Gainsight and ChurnZero

    Configure the CS platform layer.

    🎯 CTAs and Playbooks

    A CTA is triggered by a rule (health turns red, renewal in 90 days). A playbook bundles the tasks CSMs should follow, with due dates.

    ❤️ Health Scores

    Scorecards combine measures (usage, support, NPS) with weights and thresholds. Admins configure the measures, weights and Red/Yellow/Green bands.

    🗺️ Success Plans and Journeys

    Success plans track customer goals; journeys (ChurnZero Plays, Gainsight Journey Orchestrator) automate emails and tasks across the lifecycle.

    💡
    Keep CTA volume manageable. Too many CTAs cause alert fatigue.

    Module 04

    Integrations and Data Sync

    Connect systems without corrupting data.

    🔌 Integration Patterns

    PatternUse
    Native connectorCRM to CS platform sync
    API / middlewareCustom or complex flows
    Warehouse reverse ETLUsage data into the CS platform

    🗺️ Field Mapping

    Map each field explicitly and decide the source of truth and direction (one-way or two-way). ARR usually lives in the CRM or billing system.

    ⏱️ Sync Frequency

    Balance freshness against API limits. Use incremental syncs and monitor for failures.

    ⚠️
    Two-way sync on the same field without ownership rules causes overwrite loops.

Action:    Create CTA "Renewal risk"
Action:    Notify CSM manager

    Module 08

    Scenarios and Troubleshooting

    Resolve real admin problems.

    🔁 Case: Health Score Not Updating

    1. Check the usage integration job status.
    2. Verify field mappings and permissions.
    3. Re-run the sync and confirm the scorecard refresh.

    📛 Case: Duplicate CTAs Flooding CSMs

    1. Find the overlapping rules.
    2. Add suppression or dedupe logic.
    3. Reduce CTA volume and communicate the change.

    🔀 Troubleshooting

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    CS Ops analyst interview questions with model answers.

    🌱 Beginner Questions

    IQ1.What is the difference between a CRM and a CS platform?
    The CRM is the system of record for accounts and deals; the CS platform drives health scores, CTAs and success plans.
    IQ2.What is a CTA?
    A task created by a rule that prompts a CSM to act, such as renewal risk.

    🛠️ Intermediate Questions

    IQ3.How do you choose a source of truth?
    Pick the system that owns each field (e.g. CRM or billing for ARR), sync one-way and document it.
    IQ4.How do you stop overwrite loops in two-way sync?
    Assign field ownership and limit two-way sync to fields with clear rules.

    🏆 Advanced Questions

    IQ5.A workflow did not fire. How do you debug?
    Check trigger and conditions with a sample record, review logs and permissions, then retest in a sandbox.
    IQ6.How do you govern changes safely?
    Ticketed requests, sandbox build, UAT, scheduled release with rollback, and an updated inventory.
