    📋 Exam Instructions

    Please read the instructions carefully before you begin the test.

    🕒 General

    • The test consists of 5 sections: Quantitative Ability, English Ability, Logical Reasoning, Personality Inventory, and a Domain Module.
    • Each section has its own individual timer. When a section's time runs out, it will auto-submit and move to the next section.
    • You cannot go back to a previous section once it is submitted.
    • The Personality Inventory has no right or wrong answers — respond honestly and consistently.

    🖥️ Full-Screen Mode

    • The test will open in full-screen mode once you click "Start Test".
    • Do not press Esc or exit full-screen mode at any point during the test.
    • Switching tabs, minimizing the window, or exiting full-screen will be flagged as a violation.
    ⚠️ You are allowed a maximum of 3 warnings for exiting full-screen mode. After the 3rd warning, your test will be automatically submitted — no further attempts will be allowed.

    🔒 Test Security

    • Copying, pasting, cutting, right-click, and text selection are disabled throughout the test.
    • Do not attempt to use external help, screenshots, or another device during the test.

    ✅ Answering Questions

    • You must answer every question in a section before you can submit that section.
    • If any question is left unanswered, you'll see a message asking you to complete all questions before submitting.
    • Use the numbered grid within a section to jump to any question and review your answers.
    💡 Tip: Make sure you're in a quiet environment with a stable internet connection before you begin. Once started, the timer cannot be paused.
