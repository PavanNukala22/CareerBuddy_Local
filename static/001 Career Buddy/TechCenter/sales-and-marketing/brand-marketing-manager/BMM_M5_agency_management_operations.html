    Foundations

    Agency types, engagement models and who does what.

    What is agency management?

    Agency management is the discipline of selecting, briefing, contracting, directing and evaluating external partners (creative, media, PR, digital, production) so they deliver brand and business results efficiently. Agency operations are the processes, tools and governance that make this repeatable.

    💡
    Great work comes from great briefs and trusting relationships, backed by clear commercial and governance structures.

    Agency types

    TypeRoleTypical remuneration
    Creative / advertisingStrategy, ideas, contentRetainer, project fee
    MediaPlanning and buyingFee, commission, performance
    PR / communicationsEarned media, issuesMonthly retainer, project
    Digital / socialPlatforms, CRM, performanceFee, hourly, performance
    ProductionFilm, print, experientialFixed bid, cost-plus

    Engagement models

    • AOR (Agency of Record): lead partner for a defined scope.
    • Project-based: per-assignment, flexible.
    • Roster: pool of pre-approved agencies.
    • Hybrid / in-house: internal studio for volume work, agencies for ideas.

    Agency Selection & Pitching

    Running a fair, evidence-based selection.

    Selection process

    1. Define need, scope, budget and criteria.
    2. RFI to build long list.
    3. RFP to short list (3-5).
    4. Chemistry meetings and tissue sessions.
    5. Pitch or paid test project.
    6. Scored decision, reference checks, negotiation.

    Scoring matrix

    CriterionWeightEvidence
    Strategic thinking25%Case studies, insight quality
    Creative quality and effectiveness25%Work, effectiveness proof
    Team and chemistry20%Named team, fit
    Commercial15%Fee model, transparency
    Capability and process15%Tools, scale, security
    ⚠️
    Check conflicts of interest and key-person dependence; unpaid speculative pitching drives poor behaviour and bias.

    Practice

    Conflict is a risk to confidentiality and focus: require firewall commitments in the contract or select the other agency; document rationale.

    Briefing Agencies

    Writing briefs that produce effective work.

    Creative brief components

    1. Business problem and objective.
    2. Target audience and insight.
    3. Single-minded proposition.
    4. Reasons to believe and mandatories.
    5. Tone, channels, constraints.
    6. Budget, timing, success metrics.

    Brief quality test

    TestQuestion
    ClearCan the agency state the problem in one sentence?
    MeasurableIs success quantified?
    InspiringDoes it contain an insight?
    RealisticDo budget, time and scope fit?
    📌
    Briefing is a dialogue: hold a brief-back session where the agency restates the brief before work starts.

    Media and PR briefs

    Media briefs add audience definitions, reach and frequency goals, flighting, and measurement. PR briefs add messages, spokespeople, sensitivities and crisis triggers.

    Contracts & Commercials

    Remuneration, rights and risk allocation.

    Remuneration models

    ModelHow it worksWatch out
    RetainerFixed monthly fee for scopeScope creep
    Project feeFixed price per jobChange orders
    FTE / hourlyRate card times timeIncentive to inflate hours
    CommissionPercent of media spendMisaligned incentives
    PerformanceBonus-malus on KPIsClear, controllable KPIs

    Key contract clauses

    • Scope, deliverables and change control.
    • IP ownership and licence of work product.
    • Confidentiality, data protection and security.
    • Conflict and exclusivity.
    • Audit rights and media transparency (rebates, principal-based buying).
    • Termination, notice and transition assistance.
    • Payment terms and rate card validity.

    Negotiation

    • Anchor on value delivered, not just rates.
    • Benchmark rates versus market.
    • Trade volume or tenure for price or service levels.
    • Include bonus-malus for outcomes.
    ⚠️
    Always secure IP assignment or broad licence for all brand assets and talent usage terms.
    Module 5 · Part 5 of 7

    Performance Management

    Measuring creative output and partnership health.

    Scorecard

    DimensionMetric
    EffectivenessBrand lift, sales lift, effectiveness awards
    QualityBrief fidelity, craft score
    TimelinessOn-time delivery rate
    EfficiencyFirst-time-right %, cost per asset, revision rounds
    RelationshipProactivity, team stability, NPS of the partnership

    Review cadence

    1. Monthly operational check-in.
    2. Quarterly business review with scorecard.
    3. Annual 360 review (both sides).
    4. Bonus or malus settlement tied to targets.
    First-time-right % = assets approved without rework / total assets x 100

    Practice

    Diagnose root cause (brief changes, approvals, capacity), agree a recovery plan, adjust process or resourcing, and link a portion of fees to on-time delivery.

    Operations & Governance

    Processes, tools and structure for efficient agency work.

    Roles and RACI

    ActivityBrandAgencyProcurementLegal
    BriefACII
    Creative developmentARIC
    ContractCCRA
    Compliance reviewCRIA

    Workflow and tools

    • Single intake and job-numbering system.
    • Shared project management with SLAs.
    • Version control tied to the DAM.
    • Onboarding pack: brand guidelines, approval tiers, security rules.

    Structure decisions

    • Consolidate roster to reduce cost and improve integration.
    • Consider in-housing for high-volume, low-ideation work.
    • Maintain a specialist pool for innovation.
    • Audit media supply chain for transparency.

    Cases, Risks & Roadmap

    Lessons and a path to a mature agency model.

    Real-world cases

    CaseLesson
    ANA Media Transparency reportLack of transparency created rebate and conflict risks
    Unilever agency consolidationIntegration and cost gains from fewer partners
    Pepsi in-house studioInternalizing volume work can improve speed but needs creative talent

    Risk register

    • Key-person departure at agency.
    • Scope creep and unplanned costs.
    • Data breach or compliance failure.
    • Quality drift after pitch team rotates off.
    • Over-dependence on one agency.

    Roadmap & pitfalls

    PhaseFocus
    0-3 moAudit roster, spend, contracts
    3-9 moStandard briefs, scorecards, rate card benchmarks
    9-18 moConsolidation, workflow tooling, bonus-malus
    OngoingAnnual 360, market check
    ⚠️
    Pitfalls: vague briefs, focusing only on cost, no scorecard, ignoring IP and transparency clauses.
