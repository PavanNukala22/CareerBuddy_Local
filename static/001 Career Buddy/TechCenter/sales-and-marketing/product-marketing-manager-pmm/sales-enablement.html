    Sales Enablement Foundations

    What enablement is and what sellers actually need.

    🧭 What is what

    Fresher
    • Sales enablement: giving sellers the content, training and tools to have better buyer conversations and close more deals. Ramp time: time for a new rep to reach full productivity. Playbook: a guide to selling a product or into a segment.
    AssetPurposeUsed when
    Pitch deckTell the storyFirst and executive meetings
    One-pagerQuick summary to forwardAfter calls, to champions
    Demo scriptShow value in orderProduct demos
    Objection guideHandle pushbackThroughout the deal
    Battle cardWin vs a competitorCompetitive deals
    Case studyProve outcomesMid to late funnel

    📊 PMM’s role

    Fresher
    • PMM owns what to say (messaging, content, proof); Sales Enablement/Ops often owns how it is delivered (training, tools, onboarding). In small companies PMM does both.
    💡
    Enablement is judged by seller behavior and deal outcomes, not by how many assets you produce.

    Pitch Decks & One-Pagers

    Telling a clear story on slides and on one page.

    📊 Pitch deck structure

    FresherIntermediate
    • Customer problem and change in the world → why now → our solution and differentiators → how it works → proof (customers, ROI) → pricing/next steps. 10–15 slides; one idea per slide.
    • Create a short version (5 slides) for executives and a deep version for evaluators.

    🛠️ One-pager rules

    FresherIntermediate
    • One clear audience, one message, a headline benefit, three proof-backed points, a customer quote, and a call to action. Keep it forwardable without you in the room.

    🎯 Exercise

    FresherIntermediate
    • Write the slide titles (as full-sentence takeaways) for a 10-slide deck selling a reporting tool to CFOs.

    Demo Scripts & Talk Tracks

    Showing value, not features.

    🛠️ Demo structure

    Intermediate
    • Tell-show-tell: state the outcome, show it, recap the value. Start with the buyer’s top problem from discovery; show the "wow" in the first 5 minutes; avoid feature tours.
    • Script includes: setup/pre-requisites, flow, talk track for each step, transition lines, and discovery questions to ask along the way.

    🎯 Demo environments

    Intermediate
    • Use a clean demo account with realistic data, a reset process, and a backup recording for outages.
    ⚠️
    Demoing every feature loses buyers. Pick 3 jobs-to-be-done that match their priorities.

    📈 Case study

    Intermediate
    • A team rebuilt its demo around the buyer’s top 3 goals instead of a menu walk-through. Demo-to-opportunity conversion rose from 35% to 49%.

    Objection Guides & Playbooks

    Preparing reps for pushback.

    🎯 Objection handling method

    Intermediate
    • Acknowledge → Clarify → Respond → Confirm. Respond with evidence (proof points, customer stories), not defensiveness.

    📈 Common objections

    Intermediate
    ObjectionUnderlying concernResponse approach
    "Too expensive"ROI uncertainQuantify value, compare cost of status quo
    "We use Competitor X"Switching riskShow unique value and migration support
    "Not now"No urgencyTie to trigger events and cost of delay
    "Security concerns"RiskShare certifications and references

    🧪 Playbook contents

    Intermediate
    • Persona, discovery questions, value messages, objections, competitor notes, mutual action plan, case studies, and next-step templates.

    Training, Tools & Measurement

    Delivering enablement and proving impact.

    📈 Delivery

    IntermediateAdvanced
    • Launch briefings, role-plays, certification, onboarding paths, microlearning, call coaching, and just-in-time content inside the CRM or tools like Highspot and Seismic.
    • Use a content library with clear naming, tags, owners and expiry dates; retire stale content.

    🧪 Measuring impact

    IntermediateAdvanced
    MetricWhat it shows
    Content usage/adoptionAre reps using assets?
    Ramp timeNew-hire productivity
    Win rate and cycle lengthDeal impact
    Quota attainmentRevenue impact
    Certification pass rateKnowledge and readiness
    • Compare cohorts (trained vs untrained) to estimate impact.

    Advanced Enablement Strategy

    Scaling programs and linking enablement to revenue.

    🧪 Advanced topics

    Advanced
    • Segment-specific tracks (SMB, mid-market, enterprise), partner enablement, conversation intelligence (e.g., call analysis to find winning talk tracks), AI assistants for content and coaching.
    • Close the loop: field feedback → messaging updates → new training within one sprint.

    🧭 Common failures

    Advanced
    • Content nobody can find, assets that contradict messaging, training with no reinforcement, and no link to revenue metrics.

    📊 Capstone

    Advanced
    • Design a launch enablement kit for a new analytics module: deck, one-pager, demo script, objection guide, battle card, training plan, and 4 success metrics.
