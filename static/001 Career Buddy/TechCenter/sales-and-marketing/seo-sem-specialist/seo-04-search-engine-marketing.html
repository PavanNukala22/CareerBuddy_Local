    SEM Foundations

    Paid search basics and how the auction works.

    🧭 What is what

    Fresher
    • SEM: using paid search ads to appear in search results. PPC: pay per click. Impression share: share of eligible impressions you received. Ad Rank: determines position, based on bid, ad quality, expected impact of assets and context.
    • Conversion tracking: measuring valuable actions after clicks.
    MetricFormulaMeaning
    CTRClicks ÷ impressionsAd relevance
    CPCCost ÷ clicksClick price
    CVRConversions ÷ clicksPage and offer fit
    CPACost ÷ conversionsAcquisition cost
    ROASRevenue ÷ costReturn
    Impression shareImpressions ÷ eligible impressionsCoverage

    📊 SEO vs SEM

    Fresher
    SEOSEM
    CostTime and effort; no per-click feePay per click
    SpeedWeeks to monthsImmediate
    ControlLess controlHigh control over message and targeting
    Best useLong-term compounding trafficFast tests, high-intent capture, launches
    💡
    Use search ads data (queries, conversion rates) to inform SEO priorities, and SEO rankings to decide where to bid less.

    Account Structure & Keywords

    Organizing campaigns for control and relevance.

    📊 Structure

    FresherIntermediate
    • Account → Campaign (budget, goal, network, location) → Ad group (tight theme) → Keywords and ads. Separate brand, non-brand, competitor and product themes.

    🛠️ Match types and negatives

    FresherIntermediate
    Match typeReach
    ExactClosest to the keyword meaning
    PhraseContains the meaning in order
    BroadWidest; needs strong signals and negatives
    • Add negatives from the Search Terms report; use shared negative lists.

    🎯 Exercise

    FresherIntermediate
    • Design three campaigns for a project-management tool: brand, non-brand features, and competitor alternatives. State the match types and budgets.

    📈 Weekly search-term routine

    FresherIntermediate
    • Open the Search Terms report; sort by cost.
    • Add irrelevant terms as negatives (exact/phrase).
    • Add high-converting queries as exact/phrase keywords.
    • Check match type drift and ad group fit; update ad copy to mirror strong queries.

    Bidding Strategies

    Choosing and tuning bid strategies.

    🛠️ Strategy guide

    Intermediate
    StrategyOptimizes forUse when
    Manual CPCYour bidsNew campaigns, tight control
    Maximize clicksTraffic within budgetAwareness, data gathering
    Maximize conversionsConversion volumeEnough tracking; budget-led
    Target CPAConversions at a target cost~30+ conversions/month
    Maximize conversion value / Target ROASRevenue or returnValue tracking in place
    Target impression shareVisibilityBrand defense

    🎯 Good practice

    Intermediate
    • Start with a learning period of 1–2 weeks; avoid big target changes (more than 20%); feed accurate conversion values; segment brand and non-brand because their CPAs differ.
    📝
    Smart bidding is only as good as the conversion data it receives.

    Quality Score & Ad Assets

    Improving relevance and ad appearance.

    🎯 Quality Score

    Intermediate
    • A 1–10 diagnostic at keyword level from expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience. Higher quality lowers costs and improves position.
    • Fix: tighter ad groups, keyword-rich but natural ad copy, faster and more relevant landing pages.

    📈 Ad assets (formerly extensions)

    Intermediate
    AssetUse
    SitelinksExtra links to key pages
    CalloutsShort benefits (Free shipping)
    Structured snippetsLists (Types, Brands)
    CallPhone calls
    LocationNearby store info
    Price/PromotionShow offers
    ImageVisual appeal
    • Assets increase real estate and expected CTR; Google may show them when they improve performance.

    🧪 Case study

    Intermediate
    • A SaaS advertiser added 6 sitelinks, 4 callouts and tightened ad groups. Average Quality Score rose from 5 to 7, CPC fell 18%, and CTR rose 22%.

    🧭 RSA writing and Quality Score checklist

    Intermediate
    • Write 12–15 distinct headlines: keyword, benefit, proof, offer, call to action, brand; avoid repetition.
    • Add 4 descriptions that expand benefits and handle objections.
    • Match ad text and landing page headline to the keyword theme.
    • Improve landing page speed, relevance and clarity; test regularly; review Quality Score components by keyword.
