    Keyword Research Foundations

    Keywords, metrics, and tools.

    🧭 What is what

    Fresher
    • Keyword: a query people type into search. Search volume: average monthly searches. Keyword difficulty (KD): how hard it is to rank. CPC: advertiser cost per click, a proxy for commercial value. SERP: search engine results page.
    • Head, body and long-tail keywords differ in volume and specificity; long-tail terms are lower volume but usually higher intent and easier to win.
    ToolKnown for
    SemrushKeyword Magic, Keyword Gap, position tracking
    AhrefsKeywords Explorer, Content Gap, backlink data
    MozKeyword Explorer, Domain Authority, Link Explorer
    Google toolsSearch Console, Keyword Planner, Trends

    📊 Core metrics to read

    Fresher
    • Volume, KD, CPC, SERP features (snippets, People Also Ask, maps), traffic potential, clicks per search, and trend/seasonality.
    💡
    Do not choose keywords by volume alone. Relevance to your offer and intent match come first.

    🛠️ Keyword research workflow

    Fresher
    • 1) List seed topics from products, customer questions and competitors. 2) Expand in Semrush/Ahrefs/Moz. 3) Filter by relevance, volume and difficulty. 4) Check the SERP for intent. 5) Group into clusters. 6) Map to pages. 7) Prioritize and schedule. 8) Track and refine.

    Keyword Mapping & Clusters

    Turning keywords into a content plan.

    🛠️ Topic clusters

    Intermediate
    • Pillar page covers a broad topic; cluster pages cover subtopics and link back. Group keywords by shared SERP results, not just similar words.
    • Map one primary keyword (plus related terms) to one page to avoid keyword cannibalization (multiple pages competing for the same query).

    📈 Prioritization

    Intermediate
    • Score by business value × ranking probability ÷ effort. Start with high-intent, winnable terms.
    📝
    Quick wins: keywords ranking positions 4–15 that can reach the top with updates and links.

    🧪 Cannibalization checklist

    Intermediate
    • Search site:example.com "keyword" or filter Search Console by query to see which URLs rank.
    • If two pages serve the same intent, merge them and 301 the weaker one; if intent differs, separate titles, headings and internal anchors.
    • Keep one primary page per intent and link supporting pages to it.

    Competitive Gap Analysis

    Finding opportunities competitors already own.

    🎯 Gap types

    Intermediate
    AnalysisWhat it showsTool feature
    Keyword gapTerms competitors rank for and you do notSemrush Keyword Gap, Ahrefs Content Gap
    Content gapMissing topics or formatsCluster comparison
    Backlink gapSites linking to rivals but not youLink Intersect, Backlink Gap
    SERP gapFeatures you could winSnippets, videos, FAQs

    📈 Workflow

    Intermediate
    • Choose 3–5 true search competitors (not just business rivals) → export keywords → filter by relevance, KD, intent → map to pages → plan content and links.

    🧪 Case study

    Intermediate
    • A B2B software site found 220 "alternative to X" and "X vs Y" keywords competitors ranked for. It built 12 comparison pages; qualified organic demo requests rose 27% in four months.
