    Automation Foundations

    Core concepts, tools, and the customer lifecycle.

    🧭 What is what

    Fresher
    • Marketing automation: software that sends the right message at the right time based on triggers and data. Trigger: an event (form fill, page view, cart abandon). Workflow / journey: the sequence of steps. Segment: a dynamic group of contacts.
    • Lead scoring: points for fit (job title, company size) and engagement (clicks, visits). MQL → SQL: marketing-qualified to sales-qualified.
    ToolBest forStrength

    Fresher

    Example: Whitepaper download → welcome email → 2 related content emails → score ≥ 50 → alert sales.

    Building Nurture Workflows

    Logic, timing, content, and branching.

    🛠️ Workflow anatomy

    Intermediate
    • Entry trigger → wait → condition (opened / clicked / scored) → branch → action (email, tag, task) → exit.
    • Use exit rules (stop when they book a demo) and suppression (do not email customers in a sales campaign).

    🎯 Timing and content

    Intermediate
    • Match content to stage: educate (awareness), compare (consideration), prove (decision).
    • Frequency caps and send-time optimization protect engagement.

    📈 Case study

    Intermediate
    • A B2B software firm built a 5-email nurture for webinar attendees with a score-based branch. Engaged leads went to sales; others received case studies. MQL-to-SQL conversion rose from 12% to 19%.

    Tools in Practice

    How Marketo, ActiveCampaign, and Customer.io differ.

    🎯 Marketo

    Intermediate
    • Smart Lists, Smart Campaigns, programs and channels, scoring models, Salesforce sync, engagement programs for nurture streams.

    📈 ActiveCampaign

    Intermediate
    • Automations canvas, tags, deals pipeline, site tracking, conditional content, lead scoring and win-probability.

    🧪 Customer.io

    Intermediate
    • Events and attributes via API/SDK, campaigns triggered by product events, in-app and push, data pipelines, reverse ETL sources.
    📝
    Choose by use case: lifecycle B2B → Marketo; SMB sales and email → ActiveCampaign; product-led messaging → Customer.io.

    Deliverability, Testing & Optimization

    Inbox placement, metrics, and testing.

    📈 Deliverability

    IntermediateAdvanced
    • Authenticate with SPF, DKIM and DMARC; warm up new domains and IPs; keep spam complaints under 0.1%.
    • Sunset inactive contacts; honor unsubscribes; use double opt-in where needed.

    Advanced Orchestration & Governance

    Lead lifecycle, personalization, and compliance.

    🧪 Orchestration

    Advanced
    • Lead lifecycle stages with sales SLAs; round-robin routing; account-based nurture; multi-channel (email, SMS, ads sync, in-app).
    • Product-qualified leads (PQLs) from usage events feed sales and onboarding.

    🧭 Governance and compliance

    Advanced
    • GDPR, CAN-SPAM, CASL, consent management, data retention. Document naming conventions, QA checklists and rollback plans.
    • Measure program impact with holdouts: a control group receives no nurture.

    📊 Capstone

    Advanced
    • Design a trial-to-paid journey: triggers, 3 branches, scoring, sales handoff, suppression rules, KPIs and a holdout test.
