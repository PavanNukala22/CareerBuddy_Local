    Analytics Foundations

    Key metrics, tracking basics, and how GA4 works.

    🧭 What is what

    Fresher
    • Touchpoint: any interaction (ad click, email, visit). Conversion: a goal action. Attribution: giving credit to touchpoints for a conversion.
    • CAC = spend ÷ new customers. ROAS = revenue ÷ ad spend. LTV: lifetime value per customer. Payback: months to recover CAC.
    MetricFormulaExample
    CACSpend ÷ new customers$10,000 ÷ 100 = $100
    ROASRevenue ÷ spend$40,000 ÷ $10,000 = 4
    LTV:CACLTV ÷ CAC$300 ÷ $100 = 3:1

    📊 Tracking basics

    Fresher
    • UTM parameters: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign. Tag every campaign link consistently.
    • First-party cookies, pixels, server-side events, and consent determine data quality.

    Attribution Models

    Rule-based and data-driven models, with trade-offs.

    🛠️ Models compared

    Intermediate
    ModelCredit ruleBest for
    First-touch100% firstDiscovery channels
    Last-touch100% lastShort cycles
    LinearEqual splitEven journeys
    Time-decayMore to recentLong cycles
    Position-based40% first, 40% last, 20% middleBalance
    Data-drivenAlgorithm from pathsHigh volume
    💡
    Example: $100 sale with 4 touches: linear gives $25 each; position-based gives $40, $10, $10, $40.

    🎯 Limits of attribution

    Intermediate
    • Cookies expire, cross-device gaps, walled gardens (Meta, Google) report their own numbers, privacy and consent reduce visibility.
    • Attribution shows correlation, not causation.

    SQL & Spreadsheet Modeling

    Hands-on analysis and building models.

    🎯 SQL essentials

    IntermediateAdvanced
    • SELECT channel, COUNT(*) AS conversions, SUM(revenue) AS rev FROM conversions GROUP BY channel ORDER BY rev DESC;
    • Joins link touchpoints to orders; window functions (ROW_NUMBER, LAG) find first/last touch.
    • ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY user_id ORDER BY ts) = 1 gives the first touch.

    📈 Spreadsheet models

    IntermediateAdvanced
    • SUMIFS/COUNTIFS for channel totals, pivot tables for cohorts, XLOOKUP to join tables.
    • Build a model with inputs (spend, CPC, CVR, AOV) and outputs (CAC, ROAS, payback) so scenarios can be changed quickly.

    Advanced Measurement

    Incrementality, MMM, and reconciling data.

    📈 Causal measurement

    Advanced
    • Incrementality tests: geo holdouts, conversion lift, ghost bids. MMM (media mix modeling): regression on aggregate spend and sales; privacy-safe and handles offline. MTA: user-level paths, weakened by privacy.
    • Triangulate: MMM for budgets, experiments for calibration, MTA/platform data for daily tactics.

    🧪 Data quality and reconciliation

    Advanced
    • Platform-reported conversions overlap and exceed GA4 and CRM totals. Reconcile to CRM revenue as source of truth.
    • Server-side tagging, Consent Mode and modeled conversions fill gaps; monitor with alerts.

    🧭 Case study

    Advanced
    • A retailer saw Meta ROAS of 5 but a geo test showed only a 6% sales lift. Reallocating 20% of that budget to channels with tested lift improved blended ROAS by 14%.
