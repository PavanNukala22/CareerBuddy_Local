    Module 01

    Copyediting and Proofreading for Technical Editors

    Structural editing, cross-reference validation, fact-checking and keeping content consistent across distributed teams, from first edit to final proof.

    🏛️ Course Overview: What the Role Needs

    Copyediting and proofreading protect accuracy and trust. A structured workflow, careful verification and clear author communication catch what automated tools miss.

    🔍
    Copyediting
    Grammar, style, consistency.
    🧱
    Structure
    Order, headings, references.
    ✅
    Fact-checking
    Verify every claim.
    🌍
    Distributed teams
    One voice, many authors.
    💡
    Copyediting and proofreading are the last lines of defense between a draft and a reader who will act on every word.

    🎯 Learning Objectives

    By the end of this course you will be able to:

    1. Match the editing level to the content and the schedule.
    2. Perform structural edits: reorder, retitle, split and merge sections.
    3. Validate cross-references, links, figures, tables and numbering.
    4. Fact-check commands, versions, screenshots and code samples.
    5. Keep consistency across authors with style sheets and checklists.
    6. Run an effective final proofread of source and rendered output.
    7. Write clear author queries and track editing metrics.

    Module 11

    Interview Preparation

    Questions asked in Technical Editor interviews about copyediting and proofreading, grouped by level. Draft your answer, then compare with the model answer.

    🌱 Beginner Interview Questions

    IQ1.What is the difference between copyediting and proofreading?
    Copyediting improves language, style and consistency; proofreading is the final check for typos and formatting against the source.
    IQ2.How do you write a good author query?
    Be specific, give context and a suggested fix, prioritize, and keep a respectful tone.
    IQ3.How do you preserve an author's voice?
    Fix errors and clarity problems but not stylistic preferences, and explain non-obvious edits.

    🛠️ Intermediate Interview Questions

    IQ4.What is a style sheet?
    A project-specific record of decisions on spelling, capitalization, formats and terms.
    IQ5.How do you validate cross-references?
    Link checkers, anchor tests, numbering review and version checks after renames.
    IQ6.How do you fact-check a procedure?
    Run it literally in a clean environment, record versions and results, and test failure paths.

    🚀 Advanced Interview Questions

    IQ7.What source do you trust most?
    The running product or tests, then code and specs, then confirmed SMEs.
    IQ8.How do you keep distributed authors consistent?
    Style sheet, glossary, templates, linters in CI, checklists and handoff notes.
    IQ9.How do you proofread final output?
    Diff against the prior version, check rendered output, links, code blocks and accessibility.

    🏆 Expert Interview Questions

    IQ10.What metrics do you track?
    Error density, escaped defects, turnaround, query response time and link health.
    IQ11.How do you work under a tight deadline?
    Triage by risk, check commands and procedures first, use automation and log deferred items.
    IQ12.Tell me about a serious error you caught.
    Describe the error, how you found it, the fix and the prevention step.
