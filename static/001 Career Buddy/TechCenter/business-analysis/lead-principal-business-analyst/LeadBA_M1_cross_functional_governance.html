    BA Standards & Templates

    Designing a minimum viable, tailorable BA standard that squads actually adopt.

    📐 What a BA standard is

    A standard is a mandatory, checkable rule; a guideline is advisory; a template is a reusable structure. Principal BAs set a thin mandatory core and let squads extend it.

    LayerExampleOwner
    Mandatory coreStory format, acceptance criteria, traceability IDPractice lead
    Tiered extrasNFR section for high-risk changesPractice + squad BA
    OptionalJourney maps, modelsSquad BA
    💡
    Tailor by risk and size: a 2-day fix does not need a 40-page BRD.

    🧭 Rolling out a standard (step by step)

    Co-design, pilot, measure, then scale.

    1. Audit current artefacts across squads
2. Co-design the minimum core with squad BAs, QA, architecture
3. Pilot with 1–2 squads for 2 sprints
4. Measure rework, DoR pass rate, effort
5. Publish with owner, version, sunset rules
6. Review on a cadence

    Quality Gates & Baselines

    Objective exit criteria, proportional gates and controlled change.

    🚦 Quality gates

    A gate is a checkpoint with exit criteria and a named decision-maker. Strength follows risk.

    GateTypical exit criteria
    Definition of ReadyTestable criteria, dependencies known, sized
    Peer reviewChecklist passed; defects closed
    Baseline approvalImpact analysis done; owner signed
    💡
    If nobody can say which defect or decision a gate prevents, it is bureaucracy — remove it.

    📌 Documentation baselines & traceability

    A baseline is the approved reference for change control. Link objective → requirement → design → test → release both ways to run impact analysis and spot orphans.

    Joint quarterly planningObjectives, dependencies, risksQuarterly
    Scrum of ScrumsCross-team impediments2–3× weekly

    💡

    Requirement Quality Standards

    ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 attributes, review checklists and anatomy of controlled artefacts.

    ✅ Quality attributes of a good requirement

    ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 characterises well-formed requirements. Governance turns these attributes into a review checklist so quality is inspected, not assumed.

    AttributeTest questionFailing example
    NecessaryWould removing it cause a gap?Gold-plated report colour options
    SingularOne capability only?"Validate, save and email the order"
    UnambiguousOne reading possible?"Fast", "user-friendly"
    VerifiableCan a test prove it?"System shall be robust"
    FeasibleAchievable within constraints?100% uptime with no redundancy
    TraceableLinked to a need and a test?Orphan requirement
    💡
    Gate reviewers should log defect type (ambiguity, gap, conflict, duplicate); trends show where coaching is needed.

    🧾 Anatomy of core controlled artefacts

    Define the minimum mandatory fields per artefact so every squad produces comparable, auditable work.

    ArtefactMandatory coreTiered extras (higher risk)
    User storyPersona, need, value, ID, link to objectiveData impact, security notes
    Acceptance criteriaGiven/When/Then, measurable outcomeBoundary and negative cases
    NFR recordQuality attribute, metric, test methodLoad profile, RTO/RPO
    Decision recordContext, options, decision, owner, dateRisk and reversal plan
    Change requestReason, impact, approver, versionCost and schedule analysis

    🏦 Worked example: a retail bank with six squads

    Six squads used three story formats; 28% of stories failed Definition of Ready and rework consumed about a fifth of sprint capacity. The practice lead ran a 3-sprint pilot of a one-page standard.

    Baseline: measure DoR pass rate (72%) and rework hours
Pilot standard in two squads; add a 10-minute checklist review
After 3 sprints: DoR pass rate 91%, rework down by a third
Decision: adopt as mandatory core, keep tiered extras optional
Publish v1.0 with owner, change log and review date

    Governance Operating Model

    Tiering, decision rights, councils, metrics and the anti-patterns to avoid.

    🏛️ Risk-tiered governance

    Apply gates in proportion to risk × blast radius. Tier criteria should be objective and published.

    TierCriteriaRequired gates
    1 – LightSingle squad, no regulated data, reversibleSelf-check + DoR
    2 – StandardCross-squad or customer-facingPeer review + traceability
    3 – ControlledRegulated, financial, privacy, many integrationsPeer + architecture review, baseline approval, audit trail
    💡
    Anti-pattern: one heavy process for everything. It trains teams to avoid governance.

    🧑‍⚖️ Decision rights and the standards council

    Use a decision-rights matrix (RACI/RAPID) with exactly one A per decision. A council with a practice lead, rotating squad BAs and QA/architecture representation owns the standards backlog and reviews exceptions.

    DecisionRACI
    Change core templatePractice leadCouncil chairSquad BAs, QAAll BAs
    Approve tier-3 baselineSquad BAProduct ownerArchitect, ComplianceDependent squads
    Grant standards exceptionSquad BAPractice leadCouncilAudit

    📊 Governance metrics

    Balance outcome, flow and adoption measures. Avoid volume metrics such as pages produced.

    TypeMetric
    QualityRequirement-related defect and rework rate
    LeadingDoR pass rate at planning
    FlowRequirement cycle time
    AdoptionSampled audit compliance, template usage
    ValueStakeholder satisfaction, audit findings

    Cross-Squad Synchronization in Depth

    Dependency management, shared definitions and change impact across squads.

    🔁 Change impact flow across squads

    Prevent "Squad A breaks Squad B" with a short, mandatory flow.

    1. Requirement change raised with ID and reason
2. Trace links identify affected stories, interfaces, tests
3. Impact summary posted to the dependency channel/board
4. Affected squads respond within an agreed window
5. Owner decides; decision logged; baselines updated
6. Notifications close the loop

    📡 Synchronization mechanisms compared

    Choose by dependency density and number of squads.

    MechanismStrengthLimit
    Joint planning (quarterly)Aligns objectives and dependenciesHeavy for small changes
    Scrum of ScrumsFast impediment surfacingCan turn into status theatre
    Shared API/contract baselineDecouples schedulesNeeds an owner
    Enterprise glossaryPrevents definition driftNeeds change control
    Practice CoPSpreads standards by learningVoluntary participation
    💡
    Reduce dependencies at design time (ownership, contracts) before managing them with meetings.
