    BA Manager Operating Model

    Enterprise people leadership for BA practices — talent, growth, coaching, performance and retention, aligned with Workday-era talent processes.

    🏛️ What a BA Manager owns

    The Business Analysis Manager leads the BA practice (people, capacity, standards, value), not a single project. You are accountable for BA capability; program managers are accountable for delivery.

    Line management

    • Hiring, pay, promotion, performance
    • Career conversations and 1-on-1s
    • Owned by BA Manager

    Functional / dotted line

    • Day-to-day priorities on a program
    • Delivery feedback to the line manager
    • Owned by Program / Product lead

    🧩 Org models for BA practices

    ModelHow it worksStrengthRisk
    Centralized CoEBAs pooled, assigned per demandStandards, flexible capacityDistance from domain
    EmbeddedBAs report into product linesDomain depth, speedInconsistent practice, career silos
    Hybrid (recommended at scale)Embedded delivery + CoE for standards, tools, careersBalance of bothNeeds clear RACI between two bosses
    💡
    Keep manager span at 8–10 directs; use Lead/Principal BAs as pod leads and mentors above that.

    Talent Acquisition for BAs

    🎯 Competency-based hiring

    Start from a competency scorecard, not a job description. Weight each competency by what the roadmap needs in the next 12 months.

    CompetencyEvidence to look forWeight (example)
    Elicitation & stakeholder influenceConflict-resolution story, workshop design25%
    Analysis & modelingProcess / data model produced under ambiguity25%
    Domain & technology fluencyApplied domain decisions, API/data literacy20%
    Communication & facilitationExec-level summary, written clarity20%
    Coachability / leadership potentialLearning loops, mentoring examples10%

    🔄 Funnel & interview design

    hiring funnel1 Intake  role, scorecard, comp band, hiring-manager alignment
2 Source  referrals, bench, internal mobility, CoE alumni
3 Screen  30 min structured call
4 Case    ambiguous problem; assess structuring, not templates
5 Panel   behavioral + stakeholder simulation; independent scorecards
6 Debrief evidence first, discussion second, decision owner decides
    ⚠️
    Debrief traps: anchoring (senior speaks first), halo effect, and vague “culture fit”. Collect written scores before discussion.
    PointDetail
    What it isStructured, repeatable evaluation of BA candidates against defined competencies.
    Why usedStructured interviews predict performance better than unstructured chats and reduce bias.
    How to useSame questions, rubric 1–4 with anchored examples, 30-60-90 onboarding plan agreed at offer.
    Common mistakesTool-trivia screening, slow process (offers lost), contractors hired to avoid defining the role.
    Real-world usagePre-vetted bench plus time-to-offer target of 21 days cut time-to-fill from 90 to 45 days.

    🪜 Dual-track career ladder

    LevelScopeTrack
    Associate / BA ISingle feature, guidedIC
    BA II / Senior BAFull workstream, mentors juniorsIC
    Lead BAMulti-team program, standards ownerIC → Mgmt bridge
    Principal BAEnterprise capability, strategy analysisIC
    BA Manager / DirectorPeople, capacity, budget, practice outcomesManagement
    📌
    A dual track lets strong analysts grow in scope and pay without managing people.

    📋 Individual Development Plans (IDP)

    An IDP holds 1–2 prioritized goals tied to level criteria, built on the 70-20-10 mix: 70% stretch assignments, 20% mentoring/feedback, 10% formal training.

    IDPGoal      Lead a cross-team elicitation workshop series by Q3
Evidence  Facilitation feedback ≥ 4/5, signed-off scope baseline
Actions   70: lead Workshop 2  20: pair with Principal BA  10: facilitation course
Sponsor   Lead BA, review monthly in 1-on-1
    💡
    Succession planning: for each Lead role, name a ready-now and a ready-later successor and close their top two gaps.

    🗣️ Running effective 1-on-1s

    1-on-1 agenda (30 min, weekly for new BAs)5 min   check-in, wellbeing and workload
15 min  BA's topics: blockers, stakeholder issues, decisions
5 min   growth: IDP progress, feedback both ways
5 min   actions, owners, dates
    ⚠️
    Reschedule, never casually cancel: repeated cancellations tell people they are low priority.

    🧭 Coaching vs directing

    StyleUse whenExample
    DirectUrgent, compliance-critical, novice“File the change request today; here is the template.”
    CoachCapable BA, ambiguous or growth problem“What outcomes would you want from that stakeholder?”
    DelegateExpert BA, low risk“You own the workshop; update me on decisions.”

    GROW: Goal → Reality → Options → Will. Give feedback with SBI (Situation – Behavior – Impact): timely, specific, behavior-based.

    Performance Management

    Enterprise people leadership for BA practices — talent, growth, coaching, performance and retention, aligned with Workday-era talent processes.

    📈 Goals, KPIs and calibration

    Set outcome-based SMART goals linked to program results, never output volume (Goodhart’s law: a measure that becomes a target stops being a good measure).

    Good BA goal metricWhyAvoid
    Requirement defects per 100 reqs in UATQuality of analysisNumber of pages written
    Stakeholder satisfaction (pulse)Influence and clarityMeetings attended
    Scope change turnaround timeResponsivenessHours logged
    Rework cost avoidedBusiness valueStory count
    📌
    Calibration sessions compare evidence across managers to keep ratings consistent. Keep an evidence log through the year to fight recency bias.

    🛠️ Managing underperformance

    PointDetail
    What it isA staged process to restore performance or exit fairly.
    How it worksInformal feedback with examples → written expectations → Performance Improvement Plan (30–90 days, measurable targets, support, checkpoints) → HR-reviewed outcome.
    Common mistakesSurprise PIPs, vague criteria, no support, ignoring complexity differences between programs, forced distribution curves.
    Real-world usageBA missing baselines gets templates, a pairing partner and weekly checkpoints; 60 days later defect rate drops 40%.
    💡
    Use 360 feedback for development insight, not as the sole basis for pay or termination.

    🔒 Retention drivers & early signals

    DriverAction
    GrowthVisible ladder, rotations, promotion cycles twice a year
    Meaningful workMatch BAs to strategic programs, rotate away from repetitive support
    Autonomy & recognitionOwnership of workstreams, public credit, spot awards
    WorkloadCap concurrent programs; rebalance using capacity data
    Market payAnnual benchmark of bands against market
    stay interviews9-box talent reviewregretted attritionflight-risk planknowledge pairingexit insight loop

    💬 Stay interviews & flight-risk plans

    A stay interview asks current employees what keeps them and what would make them leave; an exit interview is too late. Plot talent on a 9-box (performance × potential) and track regretted attrition (loss of people you wanted to keep). Replacement often costs 50–200% of annual salary once vacancy, ramp-up and lost knowledge are counted.

    ⚠️
    A counteroffer rarely fixes the root cause; address growth, scope or workload before the resignation, and document domain knowledge so exits don’t stall programs.
