    Role Assessment

    A timed, full-screen assessment based specifically on this role.

    • 50 questions selected randomly from a 300-question role-specific bank
    • 60-minute timer — the assessment auto-submits when time is up
    • Navigate freely, answer questions, and mark questions for review
    • Questions and answer options are shuffled on every attempt

    Assessment rules

    • The assessment runs in fullscreen.
    • Do not switch tabs/windows or exit fullscreen.
    • 3 warnings for tab/window switching or leaving fullscreen will auto-submit the assessment.
    • Copy, cut, right-click and common screenshot/print shortcuts are blocked during the assessment.
