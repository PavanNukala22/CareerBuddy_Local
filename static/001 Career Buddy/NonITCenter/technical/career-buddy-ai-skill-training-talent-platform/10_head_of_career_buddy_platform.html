    Strategy, Acquisition, Integration and Metrics

    The four responsibilities of the role.

    🧭 AI EdTech product strategy

    Define the target candidate, the problem, the AI-enabled value (mock interviews, feedback, guidance) and what you will not build. Balance differentiation, quality, cost per use and trust.

    📣 B2C and B2B2C acquisition

    ModelChannelsTrade-off
    B2CContent, SEO, referrals, paid adsFast tests, higher per-user CAC
    B2B2CUniversities, employers, programmesLonger sales cycle, lower per-user CAC, large volume

    🔌 Enterprise platform integration

    NeedTypical solution
    Single loginSSO via SAML or OIDC
    Embed in a learning systemLTI or API
    Sync users and resultsWebhooks, SCIM, data exports
    Procurement trustSecurity reviews, SOC 2 or ISO 27001, DPA

    📈 Investor and growth metrics

    Track revenue, retention and efficiency with consistent definitions.

    pythondef ltv(arpu, gross_margin, churn):
    return arpu * gross_margin / churn

def payback_months(cac, arpu, gross_margin):
    return cac / (arpu * gross_margin)

    Advanced Leadership and Real-World Practice

    Scale the business and manage risk.

    🚀 Advanced concepts

    • Net revenue retention: above 100% means expansion exceeds churn.
    • AI unit cost: inference cost shapes gross margin.
    • Channel saturation: watch marginal CAC rising.
    • Configurable vs custom: avoid one-off forks.
    • AI risk: evaluation, guardrails, human escalation.

    🏗️ Real-world project

    Scenario: launch with 3 universities and grow direct B2C.

    1. Define target segments and positioning.
    2. Plan SSO and data integration for partners.
    3. Set acquisition targets by channel.
    4. Build a metrics dashboard and investor update.
    5. Review AI quality and cost monthly.

    ⭐ Best practices

    • Use one definition for every metric.
    • Tie partner pilots to outcomes.
    • Prefer configurable features over custom builds.
    • Monitor AI cost and quality together.

    ⚠️ Errors and troubleshooting

    Error

    Reporting signups as growth.

    Correction

    Report activation, retention and revenue cohorts.

    Error

    Building custom features per enterprise customer.

    Correction

    Evaluate roadmap fit and make them configurable.

    Error

    Ignoring AI inference cost.

    Correction

    Track cost per active user and optimise.
