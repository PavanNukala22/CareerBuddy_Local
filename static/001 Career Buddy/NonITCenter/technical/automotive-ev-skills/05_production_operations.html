    What is Production Operations?

    Start from zero: what a production operations team does, the 10 roles and the core vocabulary.

    🧭 What is Production Operations?

    Production operations converts plans, materials, people and equipment into finished products that meet safety, quality, cost and delivery targets, shift after shift.

    📅
    Planning
    Schedules, resources and progress tracking.
    🏭
    Execution
    Runs equipment and processes to the plan.
    🔍
    Quality
    Inspection, RCA and CAPA.
    🔧
    Maintenance
    Troubleshooting and preventive care.
    🦺
    Safety
    Protects people and equipment.
    📈
    KPIs and Improvement
    Lean/5S, data and continuous improvement.
    💡
    Every role shares three skills: Lean/5S, production KPIs and process improvement.

    Module 02 · Planning and Documentation

    Planning, Scheduling and Documentation

    Turn demand into a workable plan, read drawings correctly and keep records.

    📅 Planning and Scheduling

    Flow: Demand → Production plan → Material and resource plan → Schedule → Dispatch → Track

    PlanHorizonOwner
    Production planMonthly / weeklyPlanner
    Detailed scheduleDaily / shiftPlanner, Coordinator
    Work allocationPer shiftSupervisor
    Example · Takt time

    480 min shift − 60 min breaks = 420 min; demand 210 units → takt = 420 ÷ 210 = 2 min per unit.

    📐 Drawings, Specifications and Documentation

    • Drawings/specifications define dimensions, tolerances and materials; always use the current revision.
    • Technical documentation: SOPs, work instructions, checklists, reports.
    • Records give traceability: if it is not recorded, it was not done.

    📊 Excel/ERP and Reporting

    • ERP holds orders, stock, routings and progress; Excel handles quick analysis and trackers.
    • Good report: what happened, why, what next, with numbers.

    Safety, Equipment and Troubleshooting

    Operate equipment safely, keep it reliable and fix problems logically.

    🦺 Safety First

    Control (best → weakest)Example
    EliminateRemove the hazard completely.
    EngineeringGuards, interlocks, light curtains.
    AdministrativeSOPs, training, signage.
    PPEGloves, glasses, safety shoes.
    ⚠️
    Lockout/Tagout: isolate and lock energy before any maintenance.

    🔧 Equipment Operation and Preventive Maintenance

    MaintenanceMeaning
    BreakdownFix after failure.
    PreventiveScheduled care by time or usage.
    PredictiveCondition monitoring (vibration, temperature).
    AutonomousOperator cleaning, inspection, lubrication.

    🛠️ Troubleshooting

    Flow: Define problem → Check basics → Isolate → Find cause → Fix → Verify

    Example · Machine stops randomly

    Check power and sensors first, then compare with the last good run. Cause: loose sensor cable; fix and add it to the PM checklist.

    Lean/5S and Production KPIs

    Organise the workplace, remove waste and read the numbers.

    🧹 Lean and 5S

    5SMeaningExample
    SortRemove what is not needed.Remove old jigs.
    Set in orderA place for everything.Shadow boards.
    ShineClean and inspect.Spot oil leaks.
    StandardizeMake it the rule.Visual standards.

    Wastes: Transport Inventory Motion Waiting Overproduction Over-processing Defects Unused skills

    📈 Production KPIs

    KPIFormula / meaning
    ThroughputUnits per shift/day
    OEEAvailability × Performance × Quality
    First-time qualityGood first time ÷ units started
    Scrap / rework rateBad or reworked ÷ total produced
    On-time deliveryOn-time orders ÷ total orders
    Downtime %Stopped time ÷ planned time
    Example · OEE

    90% × 95% × 98% = 83.8%; availability is the biggest loss.

    🗣️ KPI Boards: Trend, Pareto, Action

    • Review KPIs daily at the cell in short tier meetings.
    • Pareto picks the top loss, trend shows drift, action log has owner and due date.

    Module 05 · Advanced

    Quality, RCA and CAPA

    Inspect correctly, find real root causes and make fixes permanent.

    🔍 Inspection, Checklists and Measurements

    TypeWhen
    First-offAfter setup/changeover
    In-processDuring production
    FinalBefore shipment
    • Use calibrated tools (vernier, micrometer, go/no-go) and check against drawings.
    • Record results on checklists; report defects immediately.

    🔎 Root Cause Analysis

    ToolUse
    5 WhysSymptom → systemic cause
    Ishikawa (6M)Man, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement, Environment
    ParetoPrioritise
    Is / Is-NotDefine the problem precisely
    ⚠️
    Find two causes: why it happened and why it escaped detection.

    🧩 CAPA

    Flow: Contain → Root cause → Corrective → Preventive → Verify effectiveness

    • Correction fixes the part; corrective action removes the cause; preventive action stops it elsewhere.
    • Prefer error-proofing (poka-yoke) over “retraining”.

    Expertise: Process Improvement and Leadership

    Move from solving problems to improving systems and leading teams.

    🚀 DMAIC Improvement Projects

    PhaseActivity
    DefineProblem, goal, scope
    MeasureBaseline data
    AnalyzeFind vital few causes
    ImproveTest and implement
    ControlStandardise and monitor
    Example · Reduce downtime

    Pareto shows changeover is 40% of stops. SMED cuts it from 45 to 25 min; update SOP and track on the KPI board.

    👥 Supervision and Leadership

    • Work allocation by skill, workload and safety; monitor progress against the schedule.
    • Lead with Gemba walks, clear targets, coaching and stop-the-line authority for safety and quality.

    📉 Data-Driven Operations

    • Trend analysis in Excel/Power BI to detect drift early.
    • Decisions on data, verified causes, standardised results and horizontal deployment.

    Module 07 · Practice

    Practice: A Production Line Under Pressure

    Work through a realistic scenario.

    🏗️ Scenario: Falling Output on Line 3

    Line 3 plans 1,000 units/shift but produces 820. OEE fell from 85% to 68%, scrap rose from 1% to 4%, and a conveyor stops about 12 times per shift. 5S audit score is 55%.

    0 / 0 correct

    1️⃣ Task 1 · Analyse

    ✍️ What data would you collect first?
    Model answer: Downtime log by cause, scrap by defect type, OEE split (availability/performance/quality), shift and product comparison. Build a Pareto to find the top loss.
    Q. Which OEE factor is most likely the biggest loss?
    • A Availability (12 stops)
    • B Quality only
    • C Headcount
    • D Energy

    2️⃣ Task 2 · Root Cause

    ✍️ Use 5 Whys on the conveyor stops.
    Model answer: Stops → sensor misreads → dirty, loose sensor → no cleaning or PM task → PM plan missing. Root cause: no PM/cleaning standard.

    3️⃣ Task 3 · Fix and Sustain

    ✍️ Propose actions and how you verify them.
    Model answer: Add sensor to PM and autonomous cleaning checklist, 5S the line, SOP update, train operators; track OEE, scrap and stops daily for 4 weeks.
    Q. Which KPI best proves the fix?
    • A OEE and stops per shift
    • B Headcount
    • C Parking spaces
    • D Paper use
