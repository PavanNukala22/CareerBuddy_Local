    Module 01 · Basic · Foundations

    What is Mechanical Assembly?

    From loose parts to a working, verified machine: the process, the roles and the vocabulary.

    🧭 What is Mechanical Assembly?

    Mechanical assembly joins machined, cast, fabricated and bought-in parts into sub-assemblies and finished equipment that meet function, fit, torque, alignment and cleanliness requirements. It covers fasteners, press and shrink fits, bearings, seals, gears, couplings and fluid or pneumatic connections.

    💡
    Memory aid: Read drawing → Prepare & clean → Fit → Fasten → Measure → Verify → Record.

    👷 Responsibilities and Daily Work

    Module 02 · Drawings & Specifications

    Mechanical Drawings, GD&T and Fits

    Read and apply drawings, specifications, tolerances and revision control correctly.

    📑 Reading Mechanical Drawings

    Check title block, revision, projection symbol (first/third angle), scale, units, general tolerances, material and notes. Assembly drawings carry balloons that link to the BOM; section views reveal internal fits.

    ⚠️
    Never build from an uncontrolled copy: confirm the latest approved revision with document control.

    🎯 Limits, Fits and Surface Finish

    Fit typeExampleUse
    ClearanceH7/g6, H7/h6Sliding, locating, bearings in housings
    TransitionH7/k6Accurate location, removable with force
    InterferenceH7/p6, H7/s6Permanent hubs, press and shrink fits

    Ra is average roughness. Sealing faces are typically Ra 0.4–1.6 µm, while hydraulic rods may need Ra ≤ 0.2 µm. Max interference = largest shaft − smallest hole.

    🧩 GD&T and Tolerance Stack-up

    Datum frameworks constrain 6 degrees of freedom (3-2-1). MMC/LMC modifiers give bonus tolerance as the feature departs from its worst-material size. Stack-up can be worst-case (sum) or statistical RSS (√Σt²).

    ControlTypeDatum?
    Flatness, circularityFormNo
    Perpendicularity, parallelismOrientationYes
    Position, concentricityLocationYes
    RunoutRunoutYes

    🗂️ Technical Documentation

    Keep work instructions, torque tables, inspection records, deviation approvals and as-built redlines controlled, dated and traceable. Specifications (material, coating, cleanliness, test) are as binding as dimensions.

    Module 03 · Engineering & Technical Design

    Fasteners, Fits and Assembly Engineering

    Preload, torque, joint mechanics, press and shrink fits, bearings, seals and alignment.

    🔩 Bolted Joint Engineering

    Torque-preload: T = K·d·F (K≈0.2 dry steel, 0.12–0.16 lubricated). Joint constant C = kb/(kb+km) sets how much of an external load reaches the bolt. Aim for 60–75% of proof load for reusable joints.

    Property classTensileYield
    8.8800 MPa640 MPa
    10.91000 MPa900 MPa
    12.91200 MPa1080 MPa
    💡
    Scatter in preload is ±25–35% by torque control, ±10–15% by torque-angle and ±1–5% by direct tension or ultrasonic methods.

    🧊 Press, Shrink Fits and Thermal Growth

    Press force F = μ·p·π·d·L. Shrink-fit heating: ΔT = ΔD/(α·D). Steel α ≈ 12×10⁻⁶/°C and aluminium ≈ 23×10⁻⁶/°C, so mixed-metal joints change fit with temperature.

    ⚙️ Bearings, Seals, Gears and Couplings

    🔘
    Bearing fits
    Rotating ring relative to load gets the interference fit. Heat-mount to ≤120 °C.
    💧
    Seals
    Static O-ring squeeze 15–30%, check groove finish and lubricate.
    ⚙️
    Gears
    Set backlash and contact pattern with marking compound.
    🔗
    Couplings
    Laser align; fix soft foot first, then offset and angularity.

    🧠 Advanced Technical Analysis and Design Review

    Review for DFA (fewer parts, self-locating, poka-yoke), serviceability, tolerance stack at critical gaps, fatigue (higher preload lowers bolt stress amplitude), galvanic corrosion, thread engagement (≥1×d steel, ≥1.5–2×d in aluminium) and torque access.

    Module 04 · Measurement & Inspection

    Precision Measurement and Inspection

    Instruments, calibration, uncertainty, MSA, capability, checklists and quality plans.

    📏 Instruments and Selection

    ToolTypical resolutionUse
    Vernier caliper0.02 mmGeneral dimensions
    Micrometer0.01 (0.001) mmShafts, thickness
    Dial/test indicator0.01–0.002 mmRunout, flatness, alignment
    Feeler/gap gauge0.02 mm stepsGaps, soft foot
    Torque wrench±3–4%Fastener preload
    CMM / laser trackerµm levelComplex geometry

    Rule of ten: resolution ≤ 1/10 of tolerance. Measure at 20 °C after thermal soak.

    🧪 Calibration, Uncertainty and MSA

    Calibration must be traceable with TUR ≥ 4:1. Gauge R&R below 10% is good, 10–30% is conditional and above 30% is not acceptable. Capability: Cp = (USL−LSL)/6σ and Cpk = min(USL−μ, μ−LSL)/3σ.

    📋 Inspection, Checklists and Quality Plans

    A quality plan defines characteristics, method, frequency, acceptance, records and reaction. Use first-article, in-process and final inspection, with hold points for witness checks such as torque, alignment and leak tests.

    💡
    Record the actual value, tool ID, calibration due date, date and inspector, not just 'OK'.

    Module 05 · Maintenance, Troubleshooting & Safety

    Maintenance, RCA/CAPA and Safe Work

    Preventive maintenance, equipment operation, troubleshooting, root-cause analysis and safety.

    🛠️ Equipment Operation and Preventive Maintenance

    Operate presses, torque tools, hoists and test rigs per manual and inspect daily. PM types: time-based, usage-based and condition-based. Bearing life L10 = (C/P)³ million revs for ball bearings. Availability A = MTBF/(MTBF+MTTR).

    🔍 Troubleshooting and Root Cause Analysis

    SymptomLikely cause
    1× radial vibrationUnbalance
    2× with axial vibrationMisalignment
    Bearing overheatingOver/under-lubrication, misfit, preload
    Oil leak at sealDamaged lip, shaft finish, over-squeeze

    Use 5-Why, fishbone (6M), fault tree and FMEA (RPN = S×O×D). Flow: contain → root cause → corrective/preventive action (CAPA) → verify effectiveness.

    🦺 Safety

    LOTO: prepare, notify, shut down, isolate, lock/tag, release stored energy, verify zero energy. Lift with rated slings: tension per leg = W/(n·sinθ), so shallow sling angles raise tension. Use PPE, guard moving parts and keep people out of the lift path.

    Module 07 · Practice

    Practice: Gearbox Bearing Overheating

    Apply drawing, calculation, measurement, safety and RCA skills to a realistic case.

    🏗️ Scenario

    A new gearbox runs hot and noisy at its output bearing after assembly. The build used drawing Rev B (approved is Rev C), the bearing was hot-mounted with a torch, the coupling was aligned by straight-edge, and no torque or runout values were recorded.

    0 / 0 correct

    1️⃣ Task 1 · Calculations

    ✍️ A Ø100 mm bearing seat needs 0.12 mm diametral expansion of the inner ring. Estimate the heating needed and say whether a torch is acceptable.
    Model answer: ΔT = 0.12/(12×10⁻⁶ × 100) = 100 °C, giving about 120 °C absolute from 20 °C ambient, the usual limit. A torch gives uncontrolled local overheating, so use an induction heater with temperature control.
    Q. Maximum bearing heating temperature is normally…
    • A About 120 °C
    • B About 400 °C
    • C About 800 °C
    • D None

    2️⃣ Task 2 · Investigation

    ✍️ List a safe investigation plan.
    Model answer: Apply LOTO and verify zero energy, get Rev C and compare, dismantle and inspect the bearing and seat (fit, roundness, runout), check lubricant quantity, laser-align and check soft foot, use calibrated instruments, then run a monitored test with temperature and vibration.
    Q. Which drawing do you use?
    • A The latest approved revision
    • B Any copy
    • C The oldest
    • D The one on the bench

    3️⃣ Task 3 · Fix and Record

    ✍️ Describe the CAPA and the records you would close with.
    Model answer: Corrective: replace the bearing, restore fits and laser-align with recorded values. Preventive: ban torch heating, add induction heating and alignment hold points to the work instruction, and train the team. Verify with temperature and vibration trends, and record torques, alignment readings, tool IDs, NCR and CAPA closure.

    • A Effectiveness verification
    • B Only a signature
    • C Nothing
    • D Deleting the NCR
