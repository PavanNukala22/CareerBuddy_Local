    ⚙️ Operations Foundation

    PMC Operations Coordinator: Managing secondment agreements, consultant billing approvals, monthly client status reporting, tracking deployment SLAs.

    ⚙️ Operations Foundation

    Understand how operations keeps seconded consultants, client records, billing, reporting, and service commitments controlled.

    Assignment control
    Track role, project, location, dates, approvals, and changes.
    Billing control
    Reconcile timesheets, rates, periods, approvals, and invoice evidence.
    Client reporting
    Convert reconciled operational data into concise monthly updates.
    SLA ownership
    Measure commitments, aging, exceptions, recovery actions, and closure.

    🧾 Course summary

    This specialist role is an evidence-led function. Strong performance comes from understanding the client requirement, validating actual responsibility, controlling the relevant workflow, identifying risk, and communicating a clear recommendation supported by evidence.

    🎯
    Final principle: make decisions from actual scope, evidence, outcomes, governance, and client need—not titles alone.

    🎯 What you should now be able to do

    Understand
    Explain the role and its client context clearly.
    Source / Plan
    Build a targeted specialist approach around real requirements.
    Validate
    Check scope, authority, evidence, outcomes, and risk.
    Govern
    Operate controls, approvals, reporting, and escalation effectively.
    Recommend
    Present a defensible client-facing recommendation.
