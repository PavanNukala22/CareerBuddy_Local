    Contracts & Commercial PMC Recruiter

    Build specialist PMC talent acquisition capability from fundamentals through advanced client judgement.

    🎯
    Role focus: Quantity surveyor vetting, variations and claims dispute specialist sourcing, contract administrator qualification.

    📘 Course overview

    Contracts & Commercial PMC Recruiter focuses on evidence-led identification, screening, validation, and recommendation of specialist professionals for complex PMC environments.

    🎯 Learning objectives

    • Translate specialist project requirements into evidence-based hiring criteria.
    • Source scarce technical or commercial talent through relevant project ecosystems.
    • Validate actual scope, authority, complexity, and outcomes.
    • Assess stakeholder behavior, governance exposure, and client-side judgement.
    • Prepare transparent shortlists with strengths, gaps, risks, and validation actions.

    🧩 Prerequisites

    Recruitment basics
    Sourcing, screening, interviewing, shortlisting, and candidate documentation.
    Project basics
    Scope, milestones, stakeholders, dependencies, risk, and delivery.
    Evidence mindset
    Separate verified evidence from assumptions and candidate claims.
    Professional communication
    Write concise candidate notes and client recommendations.

    📌 Beginner checklist

    • Can I explain what this specialist role actually owns?
    • Can I distinguish title from real responsibility?
    • Can I identify strong project and technical signals?
    • Can I ask for concrete evidence instead of adjectives?
    • Can I document gaps without hiding them?

    🧾 Course summary

    Contracts & Commercial PMC Recruiter is an evidence-led consulting capability. The recruiter translates the requirement, maps the specialist market, validates actual responsibility, assesses governance and stakeholder behavior, and communicates a defensible recommendation.

    🎯
    Final principle: the strongest shortlist is the one where evidence most clearly matches the client’s real project requirement.
