    The Employer Branding Specialist (RPO)

    The person who shapes how an employer is seen by the people it wants to hire: defining what makes the company a good place to work, promoting it, and protecting its reputation, on behalf of RPO clients.

    🔎 What is an Employer Branding Specialist?

    In an RPO engagement, the Employer Branding Specialist builds and runs the employer brand for the client: the story, proof and presence that make candidates want to apply and accept offers. The work sits between HR, marketing and recruiting.

    💎
    EVP developer
    Defines what the company genuinely offers, with evidence.
    📣
    Campaign marketer
    Promotes jobs and the brand through targeted campaigns.
    🌐
    Career page owner
    Turns the careers site into a page that converts.
    ⭐
    Reputation manager
    Monitors and answers reviews, and builds an honest record.

    🔄 Where Employer Branding Sits in the Hiring Flow

    Employer branding is the very front of the funnel. It shapes who notices the company, who applies and who accepts an offer, and it is checked against reviews before candidates commit.

    Module 02 · Foundation of the brand

    Candidate Value Proposition (EVP) Development

    Define the honest, specific reasons a person should join and stay, then turn them into a message every channel can use.

    💎 What is an EVP?

    The Employee Value Proposition is the set of rewards and experiences an employer offers in return for people's skills and commitment. Seen from the job seeker's side it is the candidate value proposition: what is in it for me?

    🟢 A strong EVP

    Specific, evidence-based, different from competitors, and recognized by current employees.

    🔴 A weak EVP

    Generic claims such as "great culture" or "fast-paced team", with no proof behind them.

    🧩 The five EVP pillars

    PillarWhat it coversExample proof point
    CompensationPay, bonus, equityPay bands published for every role
    BenefitsHealth, leave, flexibility26 weeks parental leave
    CareerGrowth, learning, mobility60% of managers promoted internally
    Work environmentCulture, people, flexibilityHybrid by default
    PurposeMission and impactClient products used by 5M people

    🔬 How to build one

    1. Listen: survey and interview current employees, recent leavers and candidates.
    2. Compare: review how competitors present themselves to find a distinct position.
    3. Draft: write one clear statement and three to five pillars with evidence.
    4. Test: check the draft with employees and candidates, and refine.
    5. Embed: use it in job ads, interviews, onboarding and the career site.
    ⚠️
    Do not oversell. A gap between the promise and the experience causes early attrition and bad reviews.
    Module 03 · Reach

    Recruitment Marketing Campaigns

    Apply marketing methods to hiring: know the audience, choose the right channels, tell real stories, and measure what produces applicants.

    🎯 Campaign planning

    A good campaign starts with a specific hiring need and a defined audience, such as nurses in one city or data engineers with three to six years of experience.

    Figure 3.1 · Recruitment marketing follows the candidate from awareness to application

    📡 Channels and content

    ChannelBest forContent that works
    LinkedIn and socialProfessional and mid-senior rolesEmployee posts, team spotlights
    Short videoEarly-career and high-volume rolesDay-in-the-life clips
    Job boards and search adsActive job seekersClear titles, pay range, benefits
    Events and campusesGraduates, specialist skillsPanels, hackathons
    Email and talent communityWarm and past candidatesRole alerts, news

    📊 Measure the campaign

    • Reach and engagement: impressions, clicks, video views
    • Conversion: click to application rate, apply start to finish rate
    • Quality: share of applicants who pass screening
    • Cost: cost per applicant and cost per hire by channel
    🎯
    Test, then scale. Run two versions of a message or image on a small budget, keep the better one, and repeat.
    Module 04 · Conversion

    Career Page Optimization

    Make the careers site the place where interest turns into applications: clear message, easy search, short apply process, fast on mobile.

    🌐 What a strong career page needs

    💎
    Clear EVP
    A headline and proof points in the first screen.
    👥
    Real people
    Employee stories, photos and videos instead of stock images.
    🔍
    Easy job search
    Filters by role, location and type, with clear job titles.
    ⚡
    Fast apply
    Short form, mobile friendly, no forced account.

    🩺 Audit and fix

    CheckProblem signFix
    Mobile experienceMany visitors leave on phonesResponsive layout, thumb-friendly buttons
    Page speedSlow load timeCompress images, remove heavy scripts
    Application formMany start but few finishCut fields, allow resume upload
    Job pagesLow views to apply rateAdd pay range, benefits, clear next steps
    Search visibilityLittle organic trafficDescriptive titles, job posting markup

    ✅ Optimization checklist

    • Track visits, apply starts and completed applications
    • Add a talent community sign-up for roles that are not open
    • Show pay ranges where law and client policy allow
    • Check accessibility, including contrast and screen-reader support
    • Review the page every quarter with fresh data
    💡
    Fix the biggest drop first. If most people leave at the apply form, redesigning the homepage will not help.
    Module 05 · Reputation

    Glassdoor Reputation Management

    Candidates read reviews before they apply. Manage the public record with honest responses, steady feedback gathering and action on what reviews reveal.

    ⭐ Why reviews matter

    Reviews on Glassdoor and similar sites are a trusted source for job seekers. A low rating, or silence, can reduce applications and offer acceptance. Reputation management means listening, responding and improving, never hiding criticism.

    💬 Responding to reviews

    🟢 Positive review

    Thank the reviewer, mention a specific point, and invite them to refer a friend or see open roles.

    🔴 Negative review

    Reply calmly and briefly, acknowledge the concern, state what is being done, and offer a route to talk offline.

    ⚠️
    Never argue, name individuals, reveal confidential details, or pressure employees to post positive reviews.

    📈 Build and track

    ActionHow
    Gather more reviewsInvite employees at natural moments, such as after onboarding or promotion; ask for honesty, not praise
    Track themesTag reviews by topic: pay, management, workload, growth
    Close the loopShare themes with HR and leaders, and publish what changed
    MonitorCheck ratings, review count and recommend rate monthly

    📌 Metrics to watch

    • Overall rating and trend over time
    • Percentage who would recommend the company or approve of the CEO
    • Response rate and response time to reviews
    • Link between rating, application volume and offer acceptance

    Assessment

    Test yourself on EVP, campaigns, career pages and Glassdoor. Multiple-choice questions, timed like a real screening test. Difficulty ranges from quick checks to genuinely tricky judgement calls.

    Test what you've learned
    A timed, exam-style mock test. Some questions are quick concept checks; others describe a realistic situation and ask what you would do.
    • Multiple-choice questions with a mix of concept and scenario items
    • Difficulty from Simple to Hard
    • Covers EVP, campaigns, career pages and Glassdoor

    🔒 Exam Rules & Security

    • The test runs in fullscreen with a countdown timer; it auto-submits when time is up.
    • No tab or window switching and no exiting fullscreen. After 3 warnings the test auto-submits.
    • Screenshots, copy, cut, print and right-click are disabled; refreshing or closing the page asks for confirmation.
    • Navigate freely, mark questions for review, then submit. Results and explanations appear only after submission.
    • Answers are graded on the LMS server when available, so they are never present in the page.
