    Module 01 · Foundations

    The Recruitment Analytics & Insights Lead

    The person who turns hiring data into decisions: building dashboards, tracking cost and speed, modeling how long roles take to fill, and reporting diversity honestly, so leaders can act on facts.

    🔎 What is a Recruitment Analytics & Insights Lead?

    In an RPO engagement, the Recruitment Analytics & Insights Lead collects data from the ATS, HR systems and finance, cleans it, and presents it in a way that shows what is happening, why, and what to do next. They also own the definitions, so everyone counts the same way.

    📊
    Dashboard builder
    Creates Power BI and Tableau views that answer real questions.
    💰
    Cost analyst
    Shows what hiring costs and where the money goes.
    ⏱️
    Speed modeler
    Measures and forecasts how long roles take to fill.
    🌈
    Diversity reporter
    Reports fairness across the hiring funnel.

    🔄 Where Analytics Sits in the Hiring Flow

    Analytics does not sit at one stage. It reads data from every stage of the flow and feeds the findings back to the people running each one.

    Module 02 · Delivery

    Power BI / Tableau Dashboarding

    Turn raw ATS data into clear, interactive views that let a recruiter, a manager or an executive answer their question in seconds.

    🧱 Anatomy of a good dashboard

    Start from the audience and their questions, not from the data available. Put the few most important numbers at the top, trends in the middle, and detail behind filters or drill-through.

    Figure 2.1 · KPI cards on top, trends and breakdowns underneath

    ⚙️ Build steps

    1. Connect the ATS, HRIS and finance sources, and schedule refreshes.
    2. Clean and model the data: one table per subject, consistent dates and stage names.
    3. Define measures once (for example median time to fill) and reuse them everywhere.
    4. Design visuals that match the question: line for trend, bar for comparison, table for detail.
    5. Test and publish with real users, then set access rules and a refresh schedule.

    ⚖️ Power BI or Tableau?

    🔵 Power BI

    Strong fit with Microsoft 365 tools, DAX measures and lower cost per user. Common in organizations already on Excel and Teams.

    🟠 Tableau

    Very flexible visuals and exploration, strong for detailed analysis. Often chosen by analytics-led teams.

    ⚠️
    Less is more. A dashboard with 20 charts answers no question well. Aim for one page per audience and one purpose.
    Module 03 · Money

    Cost-per-Hire Analytics

    Show what hiring really costs, where the money goes, and which choices lower the cost without hurting quality.

    💰 The formula

    Cost per hire = (internal costs + external costs) ÷ number of hires in the same period.

    🧮
    Example: $120,000 external spend (ads, agencies, tools) + $60,000 internal cost (recruiter time, referral bonuses) = $180,000. With 45 hires, cost per hire is $4,000.

    🧾 What to include

    TypeExamples
    ExternalJob boards, advertising, agency fees, assessments, background checks, travel
    InternalRecruiter and sourcer time, hiring-manager time, referral bonuses, onboarding costs
    Often missedTool licenses, employer brand spend, relocation, cost of vacancy
    📏
    Be consistent. Choose which costs count, document it, and keep it the same each period so trends are fair.

    🔎 Insights to look for

    • By channel: cost per hire and quality of hire for each source
    • By role family: engineering versus sales versus operations
    • Trend: are costs rising as hiring gets harder?
    • Cost of vacancy: what an unfilled role costs the business each week
    Module 04 · Speed

    Time-to-Fill Metric Modeling

    Define the clock, measure it correctly, find what drives it, and forecast how long future roles will take.

    ⏱️ Define the clock

    🔵 Time to fill

    Days from requisition approval (or opening) to offer acceptance. Shows how long the business waits for a role.

    🟢 Time to hire

    Days from the candidate's first application or contact to offer acceptance. Shows how fast the candidate experience is.

    📐 Modeling the metric

    StepWhat to do
    SegmentSplit by role family, level, location and recruiter
    Use the medianReport median and the 75th percentile, because a few slow roles skew the average
    Break into stagesMeasure days spent in each stage to see where time is lost
    Find driversTest factors such as feedback speed, number of interviews and pay position
    ForecastUse past cycle times by role type to estimate fill dates for open roles
    💡
    Show the range. "Most engineering roles fill in 38 to 55 days" is more honest and more useful than a single average.

    ✅ Quality checks

    • Consistent start and end dates for every requisition
    • Exclude cancelled or on-hold requisitions from the clock, and report them separately
    • Flag outliers and check the data before removing them
    • Compare against target service levels agreed with the client
    Module 05 · Fairness

    Diversity Metrics Reporting

    Measure representation and fairness across the hiring funnel, report it clearly and responsibly, and point to actions.

    🌈 What to measure

    MetricWhat it shows
    Applicant representationWho applies, by group
    Stage pass-through rateWhether groups move forward at similar rates at each stage
    Offer and acceptance rateFairness at the final steps
    Slate diversityShare of shortlists that include a range of candidates
    Source effectivenessWhich channels reach underrepresented groups
    🔍
    Look at the funnel, not just the result. A gap can appear at one stage, such as screening, even when overall hiring numbers look balanced.

    🔒 Responsible reporting

    • Collect data voluntarily, with clear purpose and consent where required
    • Follow local law and client policy on which data may be collected and used
    • Protect privacy: do not show groups that are too small to be anonymous
    • Store the data securely, separate from hiring decisions
    • Describe what the numbers show without implying cause or blaming individuals
    ⚠️
    Data is a tool for finding problems, not for setting quotas. Be clear about what is allowed and what is not in each country.

    🎯 Turning numbers into action

    • Review job ads and requirements for biased language
    • Widen sourcing channels and partnerships
    • Use structured interviews and scoring rubrics
    • Set goals, report progress every quarter, and share results with leaders

    Module 06

    Assessment

    Test yourself on dashboards, cost, time to fill and diversity. Multiple-choice questions, timed like a real screening test. Difficulty ranges from quick checks to genuinely tricky judgement calls.

    Test what you've learned
    A timed, exam-style mock test. Some questions are quick concept checks; others describe a realistic situation and ask what you would do.
    • Multiple-choice questions with a mix of concept and scenario items
    • Difficulty from Simple to Hard
    • Covers dashboards, cost, time to fill and diversity

    🔒 Exam Rules & Security

    • The test runs in fullscreen with a countdown timer; it auto-submits when time is up.
    • No tab or window switching and no exiting fullscreen. After 3 warnings the test auto-submits.
    • Screenshots, copy, cut, print and right-click are disabled; refreshing or closing the page asks for confirmation.
    • Navigate freely, mark questions for review, then submit. Results and explanations appear only after submission.
    • Answers are graded on the LMS server when available, so they are never present in the page.
